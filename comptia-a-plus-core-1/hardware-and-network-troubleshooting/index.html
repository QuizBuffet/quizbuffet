What does Corporate policies and impacts mean in Objective 5.0 hardware and network troubleshooting?

Answer: Corporate policies and impacts must be considered before changes because troubleshooting can affect users, data, security, compliance, and business operations.

Corporate policies and impacts must be considered before changes because troubleshooting can affect users, data, security, compliance, and business operations. This step prevents a technical fix from creating a business or security problem. This is the correct answer.

What does Identify the problem mean in Objective 5.0 hardware and network troubleshooting?

Answer: Identifying the problem means defining what is actually failing before changing settings or replacing parts.

Identifying the problem means defining what is actually failing before changing settings or replacing parts. Clear problem identification keeps troubleshooting focused on evidence rather than guesses. This is the correct answer.

What does Gather information from the user mean in Objective 5.0 hardware and network troubleshooting?

Answer: Gathering information from the user collects symptoms, error messages, timing, scope, and recent activity from the person experiencing the issue.

Gathering information from the user collects symptoms, error messages, timing, scope, and recent activity from the person experiencing the issue. User information often reveals what changed or how the failure appears in real use. This is the correct answer.
