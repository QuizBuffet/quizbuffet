5.0 Customers & Sales Transactions QuickBooks ProAdvisor Practice Quiz

40 exam-style questions covering 20% of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor (Online Certification) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

The Sales section in QuickBooks Online has five sub-tabs. Which sub-tab shows a list focused on invoice transactions specifically?

Answer: Invoices

The Student Guide lists Invoices as one of the five Sales sub-tabs; clicking it filters the transaction list to show invoice transactions.

After completing an invoice in QuickBooks Online, a bookkeeper wants to save it and send it to the customer by email in one step. Which save option accomplishes this?

Answer: Save and send

The Student Guide states Save and send saves the invoice and immediately sends it to the customer by email.

A coffee shop collects payment from customers at the counter at the moment of purchase. Which QuickBooks Online transaction type is appropriate for this scenario and why?

Answer: Sales Receipt, because the customer pays immediately at the time of sale and no accounts receivable balance is created.

The Student Guide states Sales Receipts are used when customers pay immediately at the time of the sale, and unlike invoices, they do not create an Accounts Receivable balance.
