Sample Questions

Which condition is a fall hazard on a construction site?

Answer: An unprotected floor opening where a worker could step through

An unprotected opening can allow a worker to fall to a lower level. This is the correct answer.

What makes a walking-working surface more dangerous?

Answer: Loose debris, wet areas, or uneven surfaces that can cause slips or trips

Poor surface conditions can cause a worker to lose footing and fall. This is the correct answer.

On site, which condition is a fall hazard on a construction site?

Answer: On site, an unprotected floor opening where a worker could step through

An unprotected opening can allow a worker to fall to a lower level. This is the correct answer.
