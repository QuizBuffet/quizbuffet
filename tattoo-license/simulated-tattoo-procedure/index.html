Sample Questions

Pigment must be dispensed from the source bottle into a

Answer: single-use ink cap placed on the procedure tray — never directly into the tube or machine

Pigment must be dispensed from the source bottle into individual single-use ink caps placed on the procedure tray; the ink cap holds the pigment for that session only; dispensing into a dedicated ink cap prevents any needle-contaminated material from entering the source bottle, maintains the sterility of the remaining pigment supply, and provides a clear, accessible pool of pigment for the needle to pick up during tattooing.

During the simulated tattoo application section, the power to the machine has been disconnected. This means the candidate demonstrates

Answer: only the needle dipping motion into the ink cap without approaching the skin

Only demonstrating needle dipping would omit the critical assessment of machine handling, skin stretch technique, machine angle, and movement across the skin — all of which are evaluated during the simulated application section.

When the examiner instructs the candidate to add another ink cap during the simulation, the new ink cap must be

Answer: a new, unused cap filled with fresh pigment dispensed from the source bottle without the bottle tip contacting the cap

When adding an additional ink cap during the procedure, the new cap must be a fresh, previously unused cap; pigment is dispensed from the source bottle into the new cap using proper no-contact dispensing technique — holding the bottle tip above the cap without touching the cap, the procedure area, or any contaminated surface; this maintains the integrity of the source pigment supply while providing fresh pigment for the additional cap.
