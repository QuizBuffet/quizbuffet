The SAP-C02 Guide

Everything you need to know before sitting for AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional

Why SAP-C02 matters in 2026

AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional is one of the most recognized credentials issued by AWS, and 2026 hiring data continues to show SAP-C02 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge — not just recall facts — across 4 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Design for New Solutions. For candidates competing in the AWS ecosystem, SAP-C02 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take SAP-C02

SAP-C02 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the AWS ecosystem. In short: Design optimized AWS solutions across organizations using the Well-Architected Framework. If you are early in your career, SAP-C02 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum — exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional exam is organized into 4 domains, each weighted by the official AWS exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Design for New Solutions at 29% of the exam — that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Accelerate Workload Migration and Modernization at 20%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 75 questions (65 scored + 10 unscored) · 180 minutes · Multiple-choice and multiple-response · Pass score 750/1000. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss — many over-study lower-weighted material because it feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill SAP-C02 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios — the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting — you can fail the whole exam by neglecting Design for New Solutions. (3) Not timing practice sessions — the exam has a real clock, and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.
