A Cosmetology License is required in every U.S. state to perform haircutting, chemical services, skin care, and nail care for compensation. State exams typically follow the NIC (National-Interstate Council of State Boards of Cosmetology) National Theory blueprint, administered by PSI or a similar testing vendor as a written exam plus a hands-on practical. The written exam covers four areas: scientific concepts (infection control, anatomy and physiology, chemistry, electricity), hair care and services (cutting, styling, color, chemical texture, hair structure), skin care and services (facials, hair removal, skin analysis), and nail care and services (manicures, pedicures, enhancements, nail disorders).

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill Cosmetology candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. Cosmetology questions, answered

What is on the cosmetology state board written exam?

Most states use the NIC National Cosmetology Theory exam or a state version of it. It covers scientific concepts (infection control, anatomy, chemistry), hair care and services, skin care and services, and nail care and services. A separate hands-on practical exam is also required in most states.

How many questions are on the cosmetology theory exam?

Typically 90 to 110 multiple-choice questions with 90 minutes to two hours, depending on your state. Ten or so items are usually unscored pilot questions.

What is the passing score for the cosmetology exam?

Your state board sets it. Most states require 70 to 75 percent on the written exam, and the practical is scored separately.

Can I take the cosmetology exam online?

The written exam is usually taken at a PSI or Prometric testing center, and some states offer remote proctoring. The practical must be done in person on a mannequin or model.

How many hours do I need before I can sit for the cosmetology exam?

It varies by state, most commonly 1,000 to 1,600 hours of approved school training. Check your state board because apprenticeship paths and reciprocity rules differ.

What happens if I fail the cosmetology exam?

You can retake it after paying the exam fee again. Most states let you retake only the section you failed and have no long waiting period, but a few limit attempts before requiring more training.
