**Full description:**
QuizBuffet is a free practice-test site for professional certifications and state licenses. It covers 50 exams with more than 33,000 exam-style questions: CompTIA (A+, Network+, Security+, CySA+, PenTest+, Cloud+, Data+, Project+), Cisco CCNA, ISC2 CISSP, CEH, eleven AWS certifications, Microsoft Azure AZ-900 and AZ-104, ITIL 4 Foundation, all six CPA exam sections, CPR/AED and BLS, OSHA 10 and 30, forklift certification, CDL Class A, FAA Part 107, the NMLS SAFE mortgage loan originator test, NASM CPT, QuickBooks ProAdvisor, real estate, cosmetology, barbering, tattoo, and micropigmentation.

Each certification is split into the same domains the real exam uses, with the same weighting, so your practice time mirrors the exam. Every question has four answers and an explanation for each one, including why the wrong choices are wrong. You can drill one domain, mix all domains, filter by difficulty, and choose a session length from five questions to the full bank. Progress is saved in your own browser and never leaves your device. There is no account, no email capture, and no paywall.
