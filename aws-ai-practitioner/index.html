The AIF-C01 Guide

Everything you need to know before sitting for AWS Certified AI Practitioner

Why AIF-C01 matters in 2026

AWS Certified AI Practitioner is one of the most recognized credentials issued by AWS, and 2026 hiring data continues to show AIF-C01 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 5 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Applications of Foundation Models. For candidates competing in the AWS ecosystem, AIF-C01 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take AIF-C01

AIF-C01 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the AWS ecosystem. In short: Foundational AI, ML, and generative-AI knowledge for the AWS Cloud. If you are early in your career, AIF-C01 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam is organized into 5 domains, each weighted by the official AWS exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Applications of Foundation Models at 28% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Security, Compliance, and Governance for AI Solutions at 14%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 65 questions · 90 minutes · Multiple-choice and multiple-response · Pass score 700/1000. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill AIF-C01 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Applications of Foundation Models. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

AIF-C01 questions, answered

How many questions are on the AI Practitioner exam?

65 questions, multiple-choice and multiple-response, in 90 minutes.

What is the passing score for AWS AI Practitioner?

You need 700 out of 1000.

How much does AWS AI Practitioner cost?

It's AWS's foundational-tier exam, $100 USD. After you pass, AWS gives you a 50% discount voucher good on any other AWS exam for one year.

Do you need to know how to code for AI Practitioner?

No. It is a foundational exam aimed at people who use or work alongside AI/ML systems (product managers, analysts, sales engineers), not the ML Engineer Associate exam's hands-on model-building content.

AI Practitioner vs ML Engineer Associate, what is the difference?

AI Practitioner is foundational and conceptual: understanding AI/ML terms, use cases, and responsible AI. ML Engineer Associate is hands-on: building, training, tuning, and deploying ML models and pipelines on AWS. Most people without an ML engineering background start with AI Practitioner.

How long is AI Practitioner certification valid?

3 years, renewed by retaking the exam or passing a higher-level AWS exam.
