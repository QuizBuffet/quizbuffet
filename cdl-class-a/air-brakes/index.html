Sample Questions

Air brake systems use a specific component to generate the compressed air that operates the brakes. According to the CDL manual, what is the basic function of the air compressor in an air brake system?

Answer: It pumps air into the air storage tanks (reservoirs)

The CDL manual states that the air compressor pumps air into the air storage tanks (reservoirs) and is connected to the engine through gears or a v-belt.

The CDL manual gives several alternate names for the brake pedal in an air brake system. Which set of names matches the manual's terminology?

Answer: Brake pedal, foot valve, and treadle valve

The CDL manual states the brake pedal is also called the foot valve or the treadle valve.

Air brake vehicles often have two distinct pressure gauges. According to the CDL manual, what does the supply pressure gauge tell the driver, in contrast to the application pressure gauge?

Answer: The supply pressure gauge shows how much pressure is in the air tanks; the application pressure gauge shows how much pressure is being applied to the brakes

The CDL manual states the supply pressure gauge tells you how much air pressure is in the air tanks, while the application pressure gauge shows how much air pressure is being applied to the brakes.
