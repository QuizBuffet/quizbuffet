8.0 Reports — QuickBooks ProAdvisor Practice Quiz

14 exam-style questions covering 7% of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor (Online Certification) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

A bookkeeper needs to find a specific financial report in QuickBooks Online and wants to access the central location where all reports are organized. Starting from the left navigation bar, which tab opens the Report Center?

Answer: Reports

The Reports tab in the left navigation bar opens the Report Center, which organizes all available standard, custom, and management reports.

A small business owner wants to quickly run a Profit and Loss report in QuickBooks Online to see income and expenses for the current month. They open the Report Center and scan the category groups. Under which Report Center group is the Profit and Loss report found?

Answer: Business Overview

The Student Guide lists the Profit and Loss report under the Business Overview group in the QBO Report Center.

A controller frequently runs the same four reports each week and wants them accessible at the top of the Report Center without scrolling through categories. The QBO Report Center provides a quick way to mark reports for easy retrieval. How does a user mark a report as a favorite in the QBO Report Center?

Answer: By clicking the green star next to the report name in the Report Center

The Student Guide states that clicking the green star next to a report name in the Report Center marks it as a favorite for quick access.
