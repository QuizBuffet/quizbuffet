About this certification

EPA 608 covers ozone depletion, the Clean Air Act, refrigerant recovery and recycling, leak detection, safety, and shipping for stationary refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment. Required under EPA Section 608 for purchasing or servicing equipment containing regulated refrigerants.
