Sample Questions

A financial services company must migrate regulated workloads while keeping audit evidence and rollback options. The team must improve the migration and modernization plan around migration assessment and readiness. What should the solutions architect recommend?

Answer: Classify each workload by an appropriate migration strategy such as rehost, replatform, refactor, repurchase, retire, retain, or relocate

The migration strategy should fit the workload's constraints, value, and modernization opportunity.

A public sector agency is replacing aging infrastructure with a governed AWS landing zone. The team must improve the migration and modernization plan around migration strategies. What should the solutions architect recommend?

Answer: Use a structured portfolio assessment that captures business criticality, dependencies, technical complexity, compliance needs, and migration readiness

A migration portfolio should be prioritized from evidence about business value, dependency risk, and technical readiness.

A financial services company must migrate regulated workloads while keeping audit evidence and rollback options. The team must improve the migration and modernization plan around application portfolio discovery. What should the solutions architect recommend?

Answer: Retain or defer workloads when dependencies, regulations, or business timing make immediate migration higher risk than value

Not every workload should move in the first wave; retained workloads still need a documented rationale.
