Sample Questions

A freehold estate in real property is characterized by:

Answer: an ownership interest of indefinite or potentially perpetual duration

A freehold estate is an ownership interest whose duration is either perpetual (fee simple) or measured by a life, both of which are indefinite as opposed to a fixed term.

A leasehold estate grants the tenant:

Answer: the right to possess and use the property for a defined period

A leasehold gives the tenant the right of possession and use for a period set by the lease, without conveying ownership.

In a limited partnership that holds real estate, the limited partners differ from the general partner in that limited partners:

Answer: risk losing only the amount of their capital contribution

A limited partner's liability is capped at the amount invested, which is the core advantage of limited partnership status.
