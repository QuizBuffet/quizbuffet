Everything you need to know before sitting for AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate

Why SAA-C03 matters in 2026

AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate is one of the most recognized credentials issued by AWS, and 2026 hiring data continues to show SAA-C03 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 4 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Design Secure Architectures. For candidates competing in the AWS ecosystem, SAA-C03 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take SAA-C03

SAA-C03 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the AWS ecosystem. In short: The most popular cloud certification for designing AWS workloads. If you are early in your career, SAA-C03 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate exam is organized into 4 domains, each weighted by the official AWS exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Design Secure Architectures at 30% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Design Cost-Optimized Architectures at 20%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 50 scored questions + 15 unscored · Multiple-choice and multiple-response · Pass score 720/1000. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill SAA-C03 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Design Secure Architectures. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

SAA-C03 questions, answered

How many questions are on the SAA-C03 exam?

65 questions total (50 scored, 15 unscored), multiple-choice and multiple-response, in 130 minutes.

What is the passing score for Solutions Architect Associate?

You need 720 out of 1000.

How much does AWS Solutions Architect Associate cost?

It's AWS's associate-tier exam, $150 USD.

Is Solutions Architect Associate hard?

It has a reputation as one of the tougher associate exams because the scenario questions often have 2 or 3 technically-valid answers and you have to pick the one that best fits AWS's Well-Architected priorities (cost, security, performance, resilience). Most candidates study for 6 to 10 weeks with some hands-on AWS time.

Solutions Architect vs Developer Associate, which should I get first?

Solutions Architect Associate is the most commonly recommended first associate cert since it covers the widest range of core AWS services at a design level. Developer Associate goes deeper into building and deploying application code, useful if your role is hands-on development rather than architecture.

How long is Solutions Architect Associate valid?

3 years, renewed by retaking the exam or passing a higher-level AWS exam.
