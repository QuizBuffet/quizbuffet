2.0 Assessment and Recognition — CPR/AED Practice Quiz

36 exam-style questions covering 12% of the CPR/AED exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the CPR / AED Certification practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

What is the first action before assessing an adult who may need CPR?

Answer: Verify that the scene is safe

The rescuer should first make sure the scene is safe before approaching and starting assessment. This is the correct answer.

A rescuer is assessing an unresponsive adult. What should a rescuer do when an adult is unresponsive?

Answer: Shout for nearby help and activate the emergency response system

An unresponsive adult should trigger help, emergency response activation, and AED retrieval. This is the correct answer.

During adult BLS assessment, what should a rescuer do when an adult is unresponsive?

Answer: Shout for nearby help and activate the emergency response system (per AHA technique)

An unresponsive adult should trigger help, emergency response activation, and AED retrieval. This is the correct answer.
