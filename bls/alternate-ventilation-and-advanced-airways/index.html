6.0 Alternate Ventilation and Advanced Airways — BLS Practice Quiz

24 exam-style questions covering 8% of the BLS exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the Basic Life Support (BLS) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

AHA Part 8 notes a key requirement for placing advanced airways during resuscitation. What is it?

Answer: Advanced airways require advanced training and are placed by trained providers

AHA Part 8 says placing advanced airways requires advanced training and is performed by trained health care professionals.

With an advanced airway in place during adult resuscitation, AHA specifies the ventilation rate as 1 breath every how many seconds?

Answer: 1 breath every 6 seconds (10 per minute)

AHA's high-quality CPR table specifies 1 breath every 6 seconds — 10 breaths per minute — for adults with an advanced airway.

To reduce the risk of gastric inflation during ventilation, AHA Part 8 recommends which approach?

Answer: Avoid giving breaths too quickly, too forcefully, or with too much volume

AHA Part 8 recommends avoiding breaths that are too quick, too forceful, or too voluminous to reduce the risk of gastric inflation.
