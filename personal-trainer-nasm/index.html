The NASM Certified Personal Trainer (NASM-CPT) credential is an NCCA-accredited certification administered by the National Academy of Sports Medicine. The certification exam consists of 120 multiple-choice questions delivered over a 2-hour window through PSI testing centers, with a passing score of 70%. Content is anchored on the NASM CPT Exam Content Outline (current 7th edition), organized into six domains: Basic and Applied Sciences and Nutritional Concepts, Client Relations and Behavioral Coaching, Assessment, Program Design, Exercise Technique and Training Instruction, and Professional Development and Responsibility. NASM's programming framework is the Optimum Performance Training (OPT) model, which sequences clients through stabilization endurance, strength endurance, hypertrophy, maximal strength, and power phases. Exam structure and difficulty

The Certified Personal Trainer (NASM-CPT) exam is organized into 6 domains, each weighted by the official NASM exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Basic and Applied Sciences and Nutritional Concepts at 46% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Program Design at 7%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill NASM-CPT candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Basic and Applied Sciences and Nutritional Concepts. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

NASM-CPT questions, answered

How many questions are on the NASM CPT exam?

120 multiple-choice questions in a 2-hour window at a PSI testing center.

What is the passing score for NASM CPT?

70% on NASM’s scaled score.

How much does NASM CPT cost?

NASM sells the exam bundled into study packages rather than as a standalone fee, typically ranging from around $600 for a self-study package to well over $1,000 for packages with live mentoring and continuing education included. Check NASM’s official site for current package pricing.

Can you retake the NASM CPT exam?

Yes. If you do not pass, NASM allows a retake after a waiting period, generally for an additional retake fee, and gives you a second attempt within your exam eligibility window.

Is NASM CPT hard?

Most candidates find the foundational sciences domain (anatomy, physiology, kinesiology, and the OPT model) the hardest part since it carries the most weight and everything else builds on it. With steady study, most candidates pass; NASM reports pass rates in the 60 to 70 percent range on the first attempt.

NASM vs ACE, which certification is better?

Both are NCCA-accredited and widely accepted by gyms. NASM leans heavily on its Optimum Performance Training (OPT) model and corrective exercise, which many trainers find gives more structured programming guidance; ACE is often described as slightly more accessible for newcomers. Employers rarely require one over the other.

How long is NASM CPT certification valid?

2 years, renewed with continuing education credits (CEUs) before it expires.
