Sample Questions

Which statement best describes a service in ITIL 4?
  • A. A means of enabling value co-creation by facilitating outcomes that customers want to achieve without customers managing specific costs and risks
  • B. A physical device that is always owned by the service consumer
  • C. A contract that guarantees every risk is removed from the customer
  • D. A department that performs technology support activities for internal users

A service helps customers achieve desired outcomes while the provider manages specified costs and risks. This is the correct answer.

In ITIL 4, who is the customer?
  • A. A person who defines requirements for services and takes responsibility for the outcomes of service consumption
  • B. A person who authorizes the budget for service consumption
  • C. A person who manages the provider's internal processes
  • D. A person who uses services every day

The customer defines service requirements and is accountable for outcomes from service consumption. This is the correct answer.

Identify the missing word(s): [?] is a set of specialized organizational capabilities for enabling value for customers in the form of services.
  • A. Service consumption
  • B. Service management
  • C. Warranty
  • D. Service offering

Service management is the specialized capability used to enable customer value through services. This is the correct answer.

Which role uses services in ITIL 4?
  • A. Supplier
  • B. User
  • C. Customer
  • D. Sponsor

The user is the person who uses services. This is the correct answer.

Which phrase describes warranty in ITIL 4?
  • A. The functionality that enables the customer outcome
  • B. Assurance that a product or service will meet agreed requirements
  • C. In this case, the person who authorizes budget for service consumption
  • D. The output produced by an activity

Warranty describes whether the service is fit for use by providing assurance about conditions such as availability, capacity, continuity, and security. This is the correct answer.

Which phrase describes utility in ITIL 4?
  • A. The activity of managing supplier contracts
  • B. The assurance that a service will meet agreed requirements
  • C. The functionality offered by a product or service to meet a particular need
  • D. The perceived benefits, usefulness, and importance of something

Utility describes what the service does and whether it is fit for purpose. This is the correct answer.

Which statement about Sponsor is CORRECT?
  • A. User
  • B. Customer
  • C. Sponsor
  • D. Service provider

The sponsor authorizes budget for service consumption. This is the correct answer.

  • A. The functionality that enables the customer outcome

Key Terms in This Domain

  • Service: A means of enabling value co-creation by facilitating outcomes the customer wants without managing specific costs or risks
  • Service management: A set of specialized organizational capabilities for enabling value for customers in the form of services
  • Service relationship management: Joint activities between provider and consumer to ensure continual value co-creation
  • IT asset management: Practice: plan and manage the full lifecycle of IT assets to maximize value, control costs, manage risks
  • Customer: Defines requirements for services and takes responsibility for outcomes of service consumption
  • Service relationship: Cooperation between a service provider and service consumer that includes service provision, consumption, and relationship management
  • Service Value System (SVS): How all components and activities of an organization work together to facilitate value creation through IT-enabled services
  • Relationship management: Practice: establish and nurture links between the organization and its stakeholders
  • Sponsor: Authorizes the budget for service consumption
  • Organization: A person or group with its own functions, responsibilities, authorities, and relationships to achieve objectives
