Sample Questions

Georgia's intangible recording tax is imposed on:

Answer: new mortgage loans secured by real property in Georgia

Under O.C.G.A. § 48-6-61, Georgia's intangible recording tax is levied on the face amount of long-term notes (mortgages) secured by real property located in Georgia, paid when the security instrument is recorded.

Georgia's real estate transfer tax is calculated at:

Answer: $1.00 per $1,000 of the sale price

O.C.G.A. § 48-6-1 imposes a transfer tax of $1.00 per $1,000 (or $0.10 per $100) of the purchase price.

Under TRID rules, the Closing Disclosure must be provided to the buyer at least:

Answer: three business days before the scheduled closing date

TRID requires lenders to deliver the Closing Disclosure at least three business days before consummation, giving borrowers time to review final terms.
