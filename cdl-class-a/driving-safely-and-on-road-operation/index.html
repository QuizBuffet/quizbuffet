Sample Questions

A driver is leaving a stop sign in a fully loaded tractor-trailer on a flat road. According to the CDL manual, what is the correct way to apply power for a smooth, controlled start?

Answer: Apply power smoothly so the vehicle moves forward without jerking

The CDL manual instructs drivers to accelerate smoothly so the vehicle moves forward without jerking, protecting cargo, drivetrain, and traction.

The CDL manual gives a specific minimum time interval that drivers should look ahead while driving. About how far ahead, in seconds, should a CDL driver scan?

Answer: At least 12 to 15 seconds ahead

The manual states a driver should look 12 to 15 seconds ahead, which is roughly a quarter mile at highway speed and one block in city driving.

Signaling intentions early helps other drivers respond safely to a CMV. According to the CDL manual, when should a driver activate the turn signal before making a turn?

Answer: Well in advance of the turn so other drivers have time to react

The manual instructs drivers to signal well in advance of the turn so others have time to react and adjust.
