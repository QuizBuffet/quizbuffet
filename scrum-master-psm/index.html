About this certification

PSM I from Scrum.org validates your understanding of Scrum as defined by the Scrum Guide. The 60-minute, 80-question online exam tests your knowledge of Scrum theory, the Scrum Team, Scrum events, Scrum artifacts, and Done. No mandatory training is required — you can take the exam directly for $200. PSM I does not expire (one-time fee, lifetime credential), which sets it apart from the rival CSM (Certified ScrumMaster) from Scrum Alliance, which requires renewal.
