Sample Questions

Commingling, as prohibited by Georgia license law, means:

Answer: depositing client funds into the broker's personal or operating account

Commingling is the illegal practice of mixing funds held on behalf of clients with a licensee's or broker's personal or business funds; all client money must be held in a designated trust account.

Under Georgia license law, a real estate salesperson must perform all licensed activities under the supervision of a:

Answer: qualifying broker licensed in Georgia

Georgia law requires every salesperson to be affiliated with and work under a Georgia-licensed qualifying broker.

To qualify for a Georgia real estate salesperson's license, an applicant must successfully complete a Commission-approved prelicense education course of:

Answer: 75 hours

Georgia requires completion of a Commission-approved 75-hour Salesperson's Prelicense Course.
