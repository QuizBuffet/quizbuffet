CDL Class A licensing is governed by 49 CFR Part 383 and administered by state DMVs under the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA). The knowledge component covers driver qualifications and disqualifications, hours of service, safe driving and hazard recognition, cargo securement, the air brake system and 7-step inspection, combination vehicles and coupling/uncoupling, the pre-trip vehicle inspection, and basic vehicle control maneuvers. A Class A CDL is required to operate any combination vehicle with a gross combination weight rating (GCWR) over 26,001 pounds when the towed unit exceeds 10,000 pounds.
