3.0 Continuous Improvement for Existing Solutions — SAP-C02 Practice Quiz

150 exam-style questions covering 25% of the SAP-C02 exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

A healthcare SaaS provider has passed initial launch and is remediating findings from a Well-Architected review. A post-incident review identifies alerting and automatic remediation strategies as the highest-value improvement area. Which recommendation is most appropriate?

Answer: Centralize application, infrastructure, and audit logs in CloudWatch Logs or Amazon S3 with retention, query, and ownership standards

A centralized logging strategy makes operational events searchable and consistent across accounts and services.

A public sector agency is improving a citizen-services application after a recent incident. A post-incident review identifies disaster recovery planning as the highest-value improvement area. Which recommendation is most appropriate?

Answer: Create CloudWatch alarms with EventBridge rules and Systems Manager Automation runbooks for well-understood failure modes

Automated alerting and remediation reduce time to detect and repair repeatable operational issues.

A healthcare SaaS provider has passed initial launch and is remediating findings from a Well-Architected review. A post-incident review identifies monitoring and logging solutions as the highest-value improvement area. Which recommendation is most appropriate?

Answer: Prioritize automation for frequent, high-risk, and high-toil operational tasks before automating rare or poorly understood exceptions

Automation investment should target recurring operational pain with clear success criteria.
