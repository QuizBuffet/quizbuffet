Everything you need to know before sitting for AWS Certified CloudOps Engineer Associate

Why SOA-C03 matters in 2026

AWS Certified CloudOps Engineer Associate is one of the most recognized credentials issued by AWS, and 2026 hiring data continues to show SOA-C03 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 5 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Monitoring, Logging, Analysis, Remediation, and Performance Optimization. For candidates competing in the AWS ecosystem, SOA-C03 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take SOA-C03

SOA-C03 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the AWS ecosystem. In short: Deploy, manage, and operate workloads on AWS. If you are early in your career, SOA-C03 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The AWS Certified CloudOps Engineer Associate exam is organized into 5 domains, each weighted by the official AWS exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Monitoring, Logging, Analysis, Remediation, and Performance Optimization at 22% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Security and Compliance at 16%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 65 questions (50 scored + 15 unscored) · 130 minutes · Multiple-choice and multiple-response · Pass score 720/1000. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill SOA-C03 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Monitoring, Logging, Analysis, Remediation, and Performance Optimization. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

SOA-C03 questions, answered

How many questions are on the SOA-C03 exam?

65 questions total (50 scored, 15 unscored), multiple-choice and multiple-response, in 130 minutes.

What is the passing score for CloudOps Engineer Associate?

You need 720 out of 1000.

How much does AWS CloudOps Engineer Associate cost?

It's AWS's associate-tier exam, $150 USD.

What was this exam called before?

SOA-C03 replaced the retired SysOps Administrator Associate (SOA-C02) exam, with expanded coverage of automation, observability, and modern operational tooling.

CloudOps Engineer vs Solutions Architect Associate, what is the difference?

Solutions Architect Associate focuses on designing systems; CloudOps Engineer Associate focuses on running and operating them day to day, monitoring, troubleshooting, deployment automation, and incident response. Pick the one that matches whether you design or operate infrastructure in your role.

How long is CloudOps Engineer Associate valid?

3 years, renewed by retaking the exam or passing a higher-level AWS exam.
