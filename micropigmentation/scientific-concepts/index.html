Sample Questions

Bacteria that require oxygen to survive are classified as

Answer: aerobic

Aerobic bacteria require oxygen to grow and reproduce; their presence in wound environments is relevant to micropigmentation because oxygen-rich surface tissues support aerobic species such as Staphylococcus aureus.

The virus of greatest bloodborne transmission concern during micropigmentation procedures is

Answer: hepatitis B

Hepatitis B virus (HBV) is the bloodborne pathogen of greatest concern in needle-based procedures; it survives on dry surfaces for up to seven days, is 50–100 times more infectious than HIV, and can be transmitted through microscopic amounts of blood on contaminated needles or surfaces.

Fungi that cause superficial skin infections are called

Answer: dermatophytes

Dermatophytes are fungi that infect keratinized tissue — skin, hair, and nails — causing conditions such as tinea (ringworm), athlete's foot, and nail fungus; their relevance in micropigmentation lies in identifying contraindicated skin conditions before performing procedures.
