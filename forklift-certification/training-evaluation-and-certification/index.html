What three elements must forklift operator training include?
  • A. Formal training, practical training, and evaluation of competency
  • B. A short quiz, a signature sheet, and permission to practice alone

Key Terms in This Domain

  • Certification document: Must record operator name, trainer name, training and evaluation dates, topics covered, and how training was implemented
  • Training combination: Required: formal instruction (lectures, videos, written material), practical hands-on training, and an evaluation of operator performance
  • Trainer qualifications: Trainer must have the knowledge, training, and experience to train and evaluate operators, including practical skill and judgment
  • Prior-training credit: If a topic was previously covered and is appropriate for the current forklift and worksite, repeat training is not required, but the operator must still be evaluated as competent
  • Refresher trigger: unsafe operation: Required when the operator is observed operating unsafely or receives an evaluation showing unsafe operation
  • Third-party training allowed: Someone other than the employer can train and evaluate, but off-site training must be supplemented with site-specific instruction; the employer still certifies the operator
  • Seat belt / operator restraint: Required when provided; prevents the operator from being thrown from the seat during a tip-over
  • Manufacturer manual: Operators must be trained in the manual’s instructions, warnings, and precautions for the specific truck model
  • Forklift-related topics: Truck-specific operating instructions, controls, engine/motor, steering, visibility, attachments, capacity, stability, inspection, refueling/charging, and operating limits
  • Evaluation every 3 years: Each operator’s competency must be re-evaluated by the employer at least once every three years
