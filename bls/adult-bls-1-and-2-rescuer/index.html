3.0 Adult BLS (1- and 2-Rescuer) — BLS Practice Quiz

This domain is part of the Basic Life Support (BLS) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

You arrive at the side of an unresponsive-appearing adult on the floor. According to the AHA Adult 1-Rescuer BLS Sequence, what is your very first step?

Answer: Verify that the scene is safe before approaching

AHA Part 3 makes Step 1 of the Adult 1-Rescuer sequence verifying scene safety so the rescuer does not become an additional victim.

When performing CPR alone on an unresponsive adult in cardiac arrest, what compression-to-ventilation ratio does AHA Part 3 specify?

Answer: 30 compressions to 2 breaths

AHA Part 3 specifies 30 compressions to 2 breaths for the single-rescuer adult ratio.

A second rescuer arrives during your resuscitation of an unresponsive adult. According to AHA Part 3, what compression-to-ventilation ratio do you use for adult 2-rescuer CPR without an advanced airway?

Answer: It remains at 30 compressions to 2 breaths

AHA Part 3 confirms the adult ratio remains 30:2 whether 1 or 2 rescuers are working without an advanced airway.
