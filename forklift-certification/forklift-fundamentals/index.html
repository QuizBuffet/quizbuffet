Sample Questions

What is a forklift as described in powered industrial truck safety training?

Answer: A mobile, power-driven vehicle used to carry, push, pull, lift, stack, or tier material

A forklift is a mobile, power-driven industrial vehicle used for moving and positioning materials. This is the correct answer.

On site, what is a forklift as described in powered industrial truck safety training?

Answer: On site, a mobile, power-driven vehicle used to carry, push, pull, lift, stack, or tier material

A forklift is a mobile, power-driven industrial vehicle used for moving and positioning materials. This is the correct answer.

A supervisor is reviewing powered industrial truck safety. Which vehicle is generally covered by powered industrial truck rules?

Answer: A powered forklift used to lift and move pallets

A powered forklift used for material handling is a powered industrial truck. This is the correct answer.
