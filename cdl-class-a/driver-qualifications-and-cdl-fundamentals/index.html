Sample Questions

A driver wants to operate a tractor pulling a 53-foot semitrailer with a gross combination weight rating of 80,000 pounds, where the trailer alone is rated at 30,000 pounds. Which CDL class is required to legally drive this combination?

Answer: Class A (combination-vehicle license)

Class A is required when the GCWR is 26,001 pounds or more and the towed unit's GVWR is more than 10,000 pounds, which fits this tractor-semitrailer.

A driver plans to haul liquid milk in a 6,000-gallon tank trailer permanently attached to the chassis, with no hazardous materials placards required. Which CDL endorsement is required?

Answer: N, the tank vehicle endorsement

The N endorsement is required to haul liquid or gaseous materials in a tank with rated capacity over 119 gallons individually and 1,000 gallons aggregate.

Federal regulations set a strict blood alcohol concentration limit for operating a commercial motor vehicle. At or above what BAC is it illegal to drive a CMV?

Answer: 0.04 percent BAC

Federal rules make it illegal to operate a CMV with a BAC of 0.04 percent or higher; any detectable amount under 0.04 still triggers a 24-hour out-of-service order.
