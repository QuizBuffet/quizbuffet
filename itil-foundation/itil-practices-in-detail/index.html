Sample Questions

Which statement best explains the continual improvement practice?

Answer: Use the continual improvement model to structure the improvement from vision through current state, target state, action, measurement, and momentum

The continual improvement model provides a structured approach for identifying, planning, measuring, and sustaining improvements. This is the correct answer.

Which key term or activity is most closely associated with continual improvement?

Answer: Ask 'Where do we want to be?' to define the target state and measurable objectives

The target state explains the desired improvement outcome and how success will be recognized. This is the correct answer.

Which statement about continual improvement is CORRECT?

Answer: Ask 'How do we keep the momentum going?' so improvement becomes ongoing rather than one-time work

Sustained improvement requires reinforcement and continued attention. This is the correct answer.
