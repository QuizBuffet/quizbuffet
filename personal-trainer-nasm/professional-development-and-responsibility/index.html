6.0 Professional Development and Responsibility — NASM-CPT Practice Quiz

100 exam-style questions covering 11% of the NASM-CPT exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the Certified Personal Trainer (NASM-CPT) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

NASM recommends that all fitness professionals maintain a focus on evidence-based practice. Which statement best defines evidence-based practice according to NASM?

Answer: The conscientious use of current best evidence in making decisions about client care

NASM defines evidence-based practice as the conscientious use of current best evidence in making decisions about patient or client care.

NASM developed a proprietary exercise training approach designed to be safe and effective for any client working toward any fitness goal. What is this proprietary approach called?

Answer: The OPT model

NASM's proprietary approach to exercise training is called the OPT model, developed with evidence-based practice as its core guiding philosophy.

A client mentions they recently recovered from a common cold that lasted about one week. According to NASM, what term best describes this type of medical condition?

Answer: Acute disease

NASM defines an acute disease as any suddenly occurring medical condition that can be treated and healed in a short period of time — which matches a resolved common cold.
