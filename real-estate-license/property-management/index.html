Sample Questions

A property manager who is hired to oversee a rental property on behalf of the owner serves primarily as:

Answer: the owner's agent, owing fiduciary duties to the owner

A property manager acts as the owner's agent and owes fiduciary duties — loyalty, care, disclosure, and accounting — to the owner as principal.

A property manager conducts a rental market analysis primarily to:

Answer: determine competitive market rents and vacancy trends to set optimal rental rates

A rental market analysis surveys competing properties' rents, vacancy rates, and amenities to help the manager set competitive rental rates that maximize occupancy and income.

In most states, a person who manages rental property for others for compensation is required to hold:

Answer: a real estate broker's or property manager's license issued by the state

Most states require a person who manages real property for others for compensation to hold a real estate broker's license or a separate property manager's license.
