Sample Questions

Before picking up a load, what should the operator verify?
  • A. The load is within rated capacity, balanced, secure, and suitable for the forklift
  • B. The load looks heavy enough to improve traction
  • C. The operator can lift first and check capacity later
  • D. The load can be carried on fork tips if the route is short

Safe load pickup begins by verifying capacity, balance, security, and suitability before lifting. This is the correct answer.

Why should operators be cautious with fork extensions?
  • A. Extensions can protrude from the backside of the load and change handling characteristics
  • B. Extensions remove the need to drive under the load
  • C. Extensions make pedestrian checks unnecessary
  • D. Extensions always increase forklift capacity

The workbook warns operators to be cautious with extensions because they can protrude from the back of a load. This is the correct answer.

How should forks be positioned before lifting a palletized load?
  • A. Inserted only under the front edge of the pallet
  • B. Properly spaced and driven as far under the load as practical
  • C. Kept above the pallet while the mast tilts forward
  • D. Narrowed together at one edge of the pallet

Proper fork position helps support the load and keep the load close to the forklift. This is the correct answer.

Key Terms in This Domain

  • Traveling with a load: Communicate with pedestrians, no riders, safe speed, follow distance of multiple forklift lengths, watch surface, slight back-tilt, sweeping turns, sound horn at corners and blind areas, lift/lower only when stopped
  • Travel height (load): 2-6 inches above the floor with a slight back-tilt for stability and visibility while traveling
  • Tip forward: Occurs when the combined CG moves in front of the drive axle (overload, load too high or too far out, sudden braking, traveling down a ramp with load forward, forks striking an obstruction)
  • Placing and stacking: Stop fully before raising, never under raised load, tilt level over the stack, fully retract forks before turning or lowering, check behind before backing, square stacks, never exceed rated capacity
  • Refresher trigger: unsafe operation: Required when the operator is observed operating unsafely or receives an evaluation showing unsafe operation
  • Manufacturer approval: Modifications or additions affecting capacity or safe operation require prior written approval from the manufacturer
  • Load center: Horizontal distance from the front vertical face of the forks to the line of action through the load’s center of gravity; most forklifts are rated at 24 inches
  • Rated capacity: Maximum load the forklift can safely handle at the rated load center and lift height shown on the data plate
  • Dynamic forces: Acceleration, braking, turning, and traveling on inclines or uneven surfaces all shift the combined CG and can cause tip-over
  • Pre-operation inspection: Daily walk-around and operational check before each shift; defects must be reported and the forklift removed from service if unsafe
