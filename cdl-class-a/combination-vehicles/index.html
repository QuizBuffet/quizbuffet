Sample Questions

Rollover is the leading cause of truck-driver deaths in crashes involving heavy combinations. According to the CDL manual, what two practices most directly help prevent rollovers in a combination vehicle?

Answer: Keep the cargo as close to the ground as possible, and drive slowly around turns

The CDL manual states the two things that most help prevent rollover are keeping the cargo as close to the ground as possible and driving slowly around turns.

An empty or lightly loaded tractor-trailer has different braking behavior than a fully loaded one. According to the CDL manual, what specific issue arises when the rig is empty?

Answer: Stiff suspension springs and strong brakes give poor traction, and it is very easy to lock up the wheels

The CDL manual states that when lightly loaded, the very stiff suspension springs and strong brakes give poor traction and make it very easy to lock up the wheels.

The CDL manual uses a specific term to describe how the rear wheels of a combination vehicle follow a different path than the front wheels through a turn. Which definition matches the manual?

Answer: Off-tracking, also called cheating, where the rear wheels follow a tighter path inside the front wheels' path through a corner

The CDL manual defines off-tracking, also called cheating, as the phenomenon where the rear wheels follow a different (tighter) path than the front wheels through a turn.
