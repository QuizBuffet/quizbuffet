Exam structure and difficulty

The CompTIA Cloud+ exam is organized into 6 domains, each weighted by the official CompTIA exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Cloud Architecture at 23% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is DevOps Fundamentals at 10%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: Maximum 90 questions · 90 minutes · Passing score 750/900 · Multiple-choice and performance-based. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill CV0-004 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Cloud Architecture. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

CV0-004 questions, answered

How many questions are on the Cloud+ exam?

Up to 90 questions, multiple-choice and performance-based, in 90 minutes.

What is the passing score for Cloud+?

You need 750 out of 900.

How much does CompTIA Cloud+ cost?

CompTIA's standard exam price is currently around $400 USD. Check CompTIA's official store for the current rate.

Is Cloud+ vendor-neutral?

Yes. Unlike AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud certifications, Cloud+ tests cloud concepts, security, and operations that apply across providers rather than one specific platform.

Cloud+ vs AWS Solutions Architect, which should I get first?

Cloud+ is a good vendor-neutral foundation if you are not yet committed to one cloud platform or need to work across multiple. If your job or target role is specifically AWS, an AWS certification will carry more direct weight with hiring managers.

How long is Cloud+ certification valid?

Cloud+ is valid for 3 years and renews through CompTIA's Continuing Education program.
