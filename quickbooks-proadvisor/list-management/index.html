3.0 List Management — QuickBooks ProAdvisor Practice Quiz

20 exam-style questions covering 10% of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor (Online Certification) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

A bookkeeper wants to see all the available lists in QuickBooks Online from a single menu. According to the Student Guide, which navigation path opens the All Lists screen?

Answer: Click the Gear icon → All Lists

Correct — All Lists is accessed from the Gear icon menu under the Lists column.

According to the Student Guide, where is the Customer Center located in the QuickBooks Online left navigation bar?

Answer: Sales → Customers

Correct — the Customer Center is opened by clicking Sales in the left navigation bar and then the Customers sub-tab.

A bookkeeper needs to open the Vendor Center to review outstanding bills and edit vendor contact information. According to the Student Guide, which left-navigation tab opens the Vendor Center?

Answer: Expenses

Correct — clicking Expenses in the left navigation bar and then the Vendors sub-tab opens the Vendor Center.
