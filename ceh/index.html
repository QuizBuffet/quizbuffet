Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) from EC-Council validates offensive security skills for penetration testers, SOC analysts, and security engineers. The current blueprint (v5.0) covers nine domains: Information Security and Ethical Hacking Overview (6%), methodologies, frameworks, controls, laws; Reconnaissance Techniques (17%): footprinting, network scanning, enumeration; System Hacking Phases and Attack Techniques (15%), vulnerability analysis, system hacking, malware threats; Network and Perimeter Hacking (24%): sniffing, social engineering, denial-of-service, session hijacking, evading IDS/firewalls/honeypots; Web Application Hacking (14%): web servers, web applications, SQL injection; Wireless Network Hacking (5%); Mobile Platform, IoT, and OT Hacking (10%); Cloud Computing (5%): container, serverless, cloud security; and Cryptography (5%). CEH is ANSI/ISO 17024 accredited and DoD 8570/8140 baseline approved for several cybersecurity roles. Often compared with OSCP (more hands-on offensive) and CompTIA PenTest+ (vendor-neutral).

The CEH Guide

Everything you need to know before sitting for Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)

Why CEH matters in 2026

Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) is one of the most recognized credentials issued by EC-Council, and 2026 hiring data continues to show CEH on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 9 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Network and Perimeter Hacking. For candidates competing in the EC-Council ecosystem, CEH is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take CEH

CEH is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the EC-Council ecosystem. In short: EC-Council's ethical hacking certification: penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, and offensive security. If you are early in your career, CEH is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) exam is organized into 9 domains, each weighted by the official EC-Council exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Network and Perimeter Hacking at 22% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Cloud Computing at 4%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill CEH candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Network and Perimeter Hacking. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

CEH questions, answered

How many questions are on the CEH exam?

125 multiple-choice questions in 4 hours.

What is the passing score for CEH?

EC-Council uses a variable cut score per exam form (typically in the 60% to 85% range) rather than one fixed percentage, since question difficulty is weighted and balanced across different exam versions.

How much does CEH cost?

CEH pricing varies significantly by package. An exam voucher alone typically runs around $1,199 to $1,999 through EC-Council or an authorized training partner, often bundled with official courseware. Check EC-Council's official site for current pricing.

Is CEH worth it without the practical exam?

The knowledge-based CEH (the one this practice test covers) is valuable on its own for DoD 8570/8140 compliance and as a resume credential, but many employers and the "CEH Master" designation specifically look for the companion CEH Practical, a 6-hour hands-on lab exam.

Is CEH harder than PenTest+?

CEH's knowledge exam leans more on breadth of tool and technique recall across a huge attack surface; CompTIA PenTest+ leans more on applied, scenario-based penetration testing methodology with performance-based questions. Many security professionals hold both.

How long is CEH certification valid?

CEH is valid for 3 years and renews through EC-Council's Continuing Education (ECE) credit program.
