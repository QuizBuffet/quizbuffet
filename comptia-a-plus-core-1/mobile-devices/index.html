In mobile device support, what is the primary purpose of Laptop hardware/device replacement?

Answer: Laptop hardware/device replacement refers to planning replacement of field-serviceable laptop components such as batteries, RAM, storage, keyboards, keys, and wireless cards while checking model compatibility, service documentation, and ESD safety.

Planning replacement of field-serviceable laptop components such as batteries, RAM, storage, keyboards, keys, and wireless cards while checking model compatibility, service documentation, and ESD safety. This is the correct answer.

In mobile device support, what is the primary purpose of Battery?

Answer: Battery refers to a removable or internal power pack that stores energy for mobile operation; replacement requires the correct form factor, connector, capacity rating, and safe handling of swollen or damaged cells.

A removable or internal power pack that stores energy for mobile operation; replacement requires the correct form factor, connector, capacity rating, and safe handling of swollen or damaged cells. This is the correct answer.

In mobile device support, what is the primary purpose of Keyboard/keys?

Answer: Keyboard/keys refers to laptop input components that may be replaced as a full keyboard assembly or individual keycaps depending on model design and damage type.

Laptop input components that may be replaced as a full keyboard assembly or individual keycaps depending on model design and damage type. This is the correct answer.
