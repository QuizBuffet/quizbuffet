Questions about QuizBuffet

Is QuizBuffet free to use?

Yes. Every practice test, drill, and progress feature on QuizBuffet is completely free, with no account required and no paywall.

Do I need to create an account?

No. QuizBuffet works without sign-up. Your progress is saved locally in your browser on the device you use, so you can pick up where you left off without ever creating an account.

Which certifications does QuizBuffet cover?

QuizBuffet covers 50 live certifications: AWS (ANS-C01, AIF-C01, CLF-C02, SOA-C03, DEA-C01, DVA-C02, DOP-C02, AIP-C01, MLA-C01, SCS-C03, SAA-C03, SAP-C02); CompTIA (220-1201, 220-1202, CV0-004, CS0-003, DA0-002, FC0-U61, N10-009, PT0-003, PK0-005, SY0-701); Cisco (200-301); Microsoft (AZ-104, AZ-900); ISC2 (CISSP); PeopleCert (ITIL 4); FAA (Part 107); NASM (NASM-CPT); American Heart Association (BLS); OSHA (OSHA 1910.178, OSHA 10, OSHA 30); Intuit (QuickBooks ProAdvisor); NIC (Micropigmentation, Tattoo); State Boards (Barber 1, Barber, Cosmetology); FMCSA / State DMV (CDL-A); EC-Council (CEH); AICPA (AUD, BAR, FAR, ISC, TCP, REG); American Red Cross / AHA (CPR/AED); Georgia Real Estate Commission / PSI (Real Estate License); NMLS (SAFE MLO). More certifications across cybersecurity, healthcare, trades, project management, and other industries are coming soon.

How are practice questions organized?

Every cert is broken down by official exam domain, with the percentage weight of each domain shown so you can focus your study time. You can also take a Mixed Quiz that pulls randomly from all domains to simulate real exam conditions.

Can I track my progress?

Yes. QuizBuffet tracks correct answers, completion status, and weak spots per domain. Visit the Progress page to see your performance across every cert and domain you have practiced.

Does QuizBuffet work offline?

Yes. QuizBuffet is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Once you have visited a cert or domain page, the questions are cached locally and you can keep practicing without an internet connection.

Are explanations included for wrong answers?

Yes. Every question shows a per-answer explanation after you submit, including why the correct answer is right and why each incorrect answer is wrong. This is the fastest way to actually learn the material rather than just memorize answers.

How is QuizBuffet different from paid practice test sites?

QuizBuffet is free, requires no account, and runs entirely in your browser with offline support. Paid sites typically gate questions behind subscriptions, sell timed exam simulations, and require account sign-up. QuizBuffet focuses on a clean study tool with thousands of domain-organized questions and instant feedback.
