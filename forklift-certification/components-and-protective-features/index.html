Sample Questions

When is an overhead guard needed on a forklift?

Answer: When the operator is exposed to hoisted or stacked objects that might fall

An overhead guard protects the operator from certain falling-object hazards when hoisted or stacked objects could fall. This is the correct answer.

On site, when is an overhead guard needed on a forklift?

Answer: On site, when the operator is exposed to hoisted or stacked objects that might fall

An overhead guard protects the operator from certain falling-object hazards when hoisted or stacked objects could fall. This is the correct answer.

A trainer is reviewing forklift protective features. What is the purpose of a vertical-load backrest?

Answer: To help prevent a load or part of a load from falling toward the operator

Load backrests protect the operator when handling small objects or unbanded units that could fall backward. This is the correct answer.
