Sample Questions

AHA Part 1 defines an 'infant' for BLS purposes. Which definition matches?

Answer: Younger than 1 year, excluding newly born infants in the delivery room

AHA Part 1 defines an infant as someone younger than 1 year, excluding newly born infants in the delivery room.

Which range does AHA Part 1 use to define a 'child' for pediatric BLS?

Answer: From 1 year of age to puberty

AHA Part 1 defines a child as anyone from 1 year of age to the onset of puberty.

An infant lies motionless on a kitchen floor. According to Step 1 of the AHA Pediatric BLS Single-Rescuer Sequence, what is the rescuer's very first action?

Answer: Verify that the scene is safe for the rescuer and the infant

AHA Part 6 makes Step 1 of the pediatric sequence an explicit scene safety verification.
