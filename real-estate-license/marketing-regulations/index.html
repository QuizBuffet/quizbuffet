5.0 Marketing Regulations — Real Estate License Practice Quiz

16 exam-style questions covering 5% of the Real Estate License exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the Georgia Real Estate Salesperson License practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

Federal law requires sellers of residential housing built before 1978 to disclose the possible presence of:

Answer: lead-based paint and provide an EPA-approved information pamphlet

The Residential Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction Act requires sellers and landlords of pre-1978 housing to disclose known lead hazards and provide buyers with the EPA pamphlet 'Protect Your Family from Lead in Your Home.'

A seller's property condition disclosure form is intended primarily to:

Answer: inform buyers of known material defects so they can make an informed purchasing decision

The purpose of mandatory seller disclosure is to give buyers information about the property's known condition so they can negotiate or decide whether to proceed.

A real estate licensee who calls a consumer on the National Do Not Call Registry to solicit a listing is:

Answer: subject to fines and may be in violation of FTC regulations

Calling a number on the National Do Not Call Registry for solicitation purposes exposes the caller to FTC enforcement and fines of thousands of dollars per violation.
