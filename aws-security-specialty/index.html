The SCS-C03 Guide

Everything you need to know before sitting for AWS Certified Security Specialty

Why SCS-C03 matters in 2026

AWS Certified Security Specialty is one of the most recognized credentials issued by AWS, and 2026 hiring data continues to show SCS-C03 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 6 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Identity and Access Management. For candidates competing in the AWS ecosystem, SCS-C03 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take SCS-C03

SCS-C03 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the AWS ecosystem. In short: Secure AWS workloads end-to-end: detection, identity, data protection, and governance. If you are early in your career, SCS-C03 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The AWS Certified Security Specialty exam is organized into 6 domains, each weighted by the official AWS exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Identity and Access Management at 20% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Security Foundations and Governance at 14%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 65 questions (50 scored + 15 unscored) · 170 minutes · Multiple-choice, multiple-response, ordering, matching · Pass score 750/1000. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill SCS-C03 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Identity and Access Management. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

SCS-C03 questions, answered

How many questions are on the SCS-C03 exam?

65 questions total (50 scored, 15 unscored), across multiple-choice, multiple-response, ordering, and matching formats, in 170 minutes.

What is the passing score for AWS Security Specialty?

You need 750 out of 1000.

How much does AWS Security Specialty cost?

It's AWS's specialty-tier exam, $300 USD.

Do you need Security+ or another security cert first?

Not required by AWS. It recommends 5 years of general IT security experience plus 2+ years securing AWS workloads specifically. Many candidates hold Solutions Architect Associate or CompTIA Security+ first, but neither is a formal prerequisite.

Is AWS Security Specialty hard?

Yes, it goes deep into a narrow domain, IAM edge cases, KMS key policies, incident response runbooks, and multi-account governance, rather than testing broad AWS knowledge. Prior hands-on security work on AWS makes a large difference.

How long is Security Specialty certification valid?

3 years, renewed by retaking the exam or passing a higher-level AWS exam.
