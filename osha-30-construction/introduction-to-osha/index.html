40 exam-style questions covering 7% of the OSHA 30 exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the OSHA 30-Hour Construction practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

Why was OSHA established?

Answer: To help assure safe and healthful working conditions for workers

OSHA’s core purpose is to help assure safe and healthful working conditions through standards, enforcement, training, outreach, education, and assistance. This is the correct answer.

On site, why was OSHA established?

Answer: On site, to help assure safe and healthful working conditions for workers

OSHA’s core purpose is to help assure safe and healthful working conditions through standards, enforcement, training, outreach, education, and assistance. This is the correct answer.

A construction crew is reviewing OSHA responsibilities. A worker wants information about chemical hazards used on the jobsite. Which right applies?

Answer: The right to receive information and training about workplace hazards

Workers have rights to hazard information and training they can understand. This is the correct answer.
