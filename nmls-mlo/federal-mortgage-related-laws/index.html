Sample Questions

RESPA was enacted by Congress in 1974 to address rising costs in real estate transactions. Lenders, brokers, and servicers must comply with its disclosure and anti-kickback provisions. What is the primary purpose of RESPA?

Answer: To provide consumers disclosures about settlement costs and to prohibit kickbacks that increase those costs

Correct — RESPA's stated purpose under 12 USC 2601 is to give consumers settlement-cost disclosures and to eliminate kickbacks and unearned fees that inflate those costs.

Under Regulation X, classification as a mortgage broker matters because RESPA's anti-kickback rules attach to settlement service providers. The definition draws a line around persons who arrange but do not fund the loan. Under RESPA, a 'mortgage broker' is best defined as:

Answer: A person, other than an employee of a lender, who renders origination services and serves as an intermediary between a borrower and a lender

Correct — Regulation X defines a mortgage broker as a person, other than an employee of the lender, who renders origination services and acts as an intermediary between borrower and lender.

Section 8 of RESPA targets unearned fees that inflate the cost of settlement services. A common example is splitting a fee with a referral source who performed no actual work. Section 8 of RESPA prohibits:

Answer: Giving or accepting any thing of value for the referral of settlement service business involving a federally related mortgage loan

Correct — 12 USC 2607 prohibits giving or accepting fees, kickbacks, or things of value for referrals of settlement service business on federally related mortgage loans.
