Sample Questions

The SAFE Mortgage Licensing Act of 2008 established minimum standards for mortgage loan originator licensing and registration nationwide. The SAFE Act was enacted primarily to:

Answer: Protect consumers and reduce fraud by establishing minimum standards for MLO licensing and registration through the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System

Correct — the SAFE Act's stated purpose is consumer protection through minimum licensing standards and the NMLS.

Candidates for state MLO licensure must pass a written examination covering federal and state law. The SAFE Act sets a minimum passing standard. The minimum passing score on the SAFE Act National Test is:

Answer: 75% correct

Correct — 12 USC 5107 establishes a 75% minimum passing score.

Licensed MLOs must complete continuing education annually to maintain their license. The SAFE Act specifies mandatory content areas within the annual requirement. The minimum annual continuing education requirement under the SAFE Act is:

Answer: 8 hours per year, including 3 hours of federal law, 2 hours of ethics, 2 hours of non-traditional lending, and 1 hour of elective

Correct — 12 USC 5106 requires 8 annual CE hours with those content minimums.
