7.0 Banking, Feeds & Reconciliation — QuickBooks ProAdvisor Practice Quiz

30 exam-style questions covering 15% of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor (Online Certification) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

Which file formats can be used to manually upload bank transactions to QuickBooks Online when a financial institution does not support automatic bank feeds?

Answer: .CSV, .QFX, .QBO, and .OFX

The Student Guide states QBO supports manual transaction uploads in .CSV, .QFX, .QBO, and .OFX file formats.

The Banking Center in QuickBooks Online has three main tabs for managing downloaded transactions. Which set correctly names all three?

Answer: For Review, Reviewed, Excluded.

The Student Guide identifies the three Banking Center tabs as For Review (transactions awaiting action), Reviewed (accepted/matched transactions), and Excluded (transactions removed from consideration).

The For Review tab in the Banking Center contains two sub-tabs. What are they?

Answer: All and Recognized.

The Student Guide identifies the two sub-tabs within For Review as All (every downloaded transaction awaiting action) and Recognized (transactions that QBO has already identified a likely category or match for).
