6.0 Alternate Ventilation and Advanced Airways BLS Practice Quiz

24 exam-style questions covering 8% of the BLS exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the Basic Life Support (BLS) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

AHA Part 8 notes a key requirement for placing advanced airways during resuscitation. What is it?
  • A. Advanced airways can be placed by any bystander without training
  • B. Advanced airways are only available in hospitals
  • C. Advanced airways are forbidden during CPR
  • D. Advanced airways require advanced training and are placed by trained providers

AHA Part 8 says placing advanced airways requires advanced training and is performed by trained health care professionals.

With an advanced airway in place during adult resuscitation, AHA specifies the ventilation rate as 1 breath every how many seconds?
  • A. 1 breath every 6 seconds (10 per minute)
  • B. Pediatric rate: 1 breath every 2 to 3 seconds for children and infants
  • C. Provide no breaths; the advanced airway delivers oxygen passively
  • D. Deliver 1 breath every 3 minutes to avoid hyperventilation

AHA's high-quality CPR table specifies 1 breath every 6 seconds, 10 breaths per minute, for adults with an advanced airway.

While giving mouth-to-mouth breaths to an adult, the first breath produces no chest rise. According to AHA Part 8, what should you do before the second breath?
  • A. Press much harder on the second attempt
  • B. Skip the second breath and resume compressions
  • C. Repeat the head tilt-chin lift to reopen the airway, then try again
  • D. Insert a finger deep into the throat to feel for an obstruction

AHA Part 8 directs the rescuer to repeat the head tilt-chin lift if the chest does not rise, then try again to give a breath that makes the chest rise.

AHA Part 8 describes mouth-to-nose breathing as an alternative. In which situation is it appropriate?
  • A. It is always preferred over mouth-to-mouth for adults
  • B. When mouth-to-mouth breaths cannot be performed, for example, when the mouth cannot be opened
  • C. Only for children who cannot seal their lips
  • D. Only for infants, never adults

AHA Part 8 says mouth-to-nose breathing may be used when mouth-to-mouth cannot be performed, for example, if the person's mouth cannot be opened or the rescuer cannot make a good seal.

AHA Part 8 describes a modification used when giving breaths to an infant without a barrier device. Which technique matches?
  • A. Cover both the infant's mouth and nose with your mouth, then give a breath
  • B. Cover only the infant's nose and seal the mouth
  • C. Deliver breaths by blowing on the infant's forehead
  • D. An infant cannot receive mouth-to-mouth without a barrier

AHA Part 8 says for infants, you can cover both the mouth and nose with your mouth because of the infant's small face, and give breaths.

AHA Part 8 lists examples of advanced airways. Which set matches?
  • A. Nasopharyngeal airway and oral airway only
  • B. Only an endotracheal tube placed by a physician
  • C. Pocket mask and face shield only
  • D. Endotracheal tube and supraglottic airways

AHA Part 8 lists the endotracheal tube and supraglottic airway devices as examples of advanced airways that require specialized training.

AHA Part 8 warns specifically about providing too many breaths during CPR with an advanced airway. Which consequence does the manual highlight?
  • A. Excess ventilation increases cardiac output and improves survival
  • B. Too many breaths only affect blood pressure after ROSC
  • C. Extra breaths have no effect on circulation during CPR
  • D. Hyperventilation increases intrathoracic pressure, reduces venous return, and decreases cardiac output

AHA warns that hyperventilation during CPR increases intrathoracic pressure, impairs venous return to the heart, and decreases cardiac output during compressions.

AHA Part 8 explains what changes about CPR technique once an advanced airway is placed in an adult. Which statement matches?
  • A. Compressions pause every 30 to allow 2 breaths, the same as before the airway
  • B. CPR stops entirely until the airway is confirmed secure
  • C. Compressions can be continuous without pausing for breaths
  • D. Ventilation rate doubles to 20 breaths per minute

AHA Part 8 says that with an advanced airway in place, the Compressor can give continuous compressions without pausing for breaths.
