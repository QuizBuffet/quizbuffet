6.0 Choking and Airway Obstruction — CPR/AED Practice Quiz

36 exam-style questions covering 12% of the CPR/AED exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the CPR / AED Certification practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

An adult at a restaurant suddenly stops eating, clutches the front of the neck with both hands, and looks panicked. AHA Part 11 calls this gesture by what name?

Answer: The universal choking sign

AHA Part 11 names the universal choking sign — clutching the neck with both hands — as the indicator that someone is choking.

An adult clutches their throat, nods 'yes' to 'Are you choking?', and cannot speak. According to AHA Part 11's Adult FBAO sequence, what should you do first?

Answer: Give 5 forceful back blows between the shoulder blades, then 5 abdominal thrusts if needed

AHA Part 11 directs the rescuer to deliver 5 forceful back blows between the shoulder blades; if those do not relieve the choking, perform 5 abdominal thrusts.

AHA Part 11 describes how to deliver back blows for choking relief in a responsive adult or child. Which technique matches?

Answer: Use the heel of your hand to forcefully strike between the shoulder blades

AHA Part 11 specifies using the heel of the hand to forcefully strike the person's back between their shoulder blades.
