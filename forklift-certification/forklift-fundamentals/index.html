Sample Questions

What is a forklift as described in powered industrial truck safety training?
  • A. A mobile, power-driven vehicle used to carry, push, pull, lift, stack, or tier material
  • B. A hand-carried tool used only for lifting small boxes
  • C. A fixed conveyor used only to move material along one path
  • D. A passenger vehicle used primarily for transporting employees

A forklift is a mobile, power-driven industrial vehicle used for moving and positioning materials. This is the correct answer.

On site, what is a forklift as described in powered industrial truck safety training?
  • A. On site, a mobile, power-driven vehicle used to carry, push, pull, lift, stack, or tier material
  • B. On site, a fixed conveyor used only to move material along one path
  • C. On site, a passenger vehicle used primarily for transporting employees
  • D. On site, a hand-carried tool used only for lifting small boxes

A forklift is a mobile, power-driven industrial vehicle used for moving and positioning materials. This is the correct answer.

Which task is a typical forklift use?
  • A. Lifting people without an approved work platform and controls
  • B. Tiering pallets in a warehouse or loading materials at a construction site
  • C. Using the mast as a crane without considering capacity or manufacturer limits
  • D. Transporting workers as passengers on the forks

Forklifts are commonly used to lift, stack, tier, load, unload, and move materials. This is the correct answer.

Which item is identified as not covered by 29 CFR 1910.178 in the workbook?
  • A. A counterbalanced forklift used for material handling
  • B. Vehicles intended primarily for earth moving, such as skid steers
  • C. A forklift equipped with forks for tiering pallets
  • D. A lift truck used to unload trailers

The workbook identifies earthmoving vehicles such as skid steers as outside 29 CFR 1910.178 coverage. This is the correct answer.

During forklift operator certification training, why are forklifts a significant safety concern?
  • A. They are safer than all other vehicles because they move indoors
  • B. They are harmless when moving slowly
  • C. They are widely used around workers and pedestrians and can create serious struck-by, tip-over, and crushing hazards
  • D. They cannot tip over when unloaded

Forklifts operate near people and materials, so safe operation and pedestrian protection are critical. This is the correct answer.

Which statement best describes forklift safety training purpose?
  • A. To teach operators to bypass manufacturer instructions
  • B. To replace hands-on evaluation with a short quiz only
  • C. To allow any worker to operate any forklift without site-specific instruction
  • D. To help operators and affected workers recognize hazards and use safe work practices
