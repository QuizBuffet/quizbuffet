Sample Questions

Which condition is an electrical hazard on a construction site?
  • A. An exposed energized conductor that workers could contact
  • B. A properly stored nonconductive tool
  • C. A safety sign that warns workers about energized equipment
  • D. A dry wooden barricade placed to keep workers away

Exposed energized conductors can shock or electrocute workers. This is the correct answer.

Which worker action increases electrocution risk?
  • A. Using metal tools near energized electrical components
  • B. On the project, a properly stored nonconductive tool
  • C. On the project, a safety sign that warns workers about energized equipment
  • D. On the project, a dry wooden barricade placed to keep workers away

Conductive tools can create a path for electrical current. This is the correct answer.

Why is damaged insulation on a cord dangerous?
  • A. On site, a safety sign that warns workers about energized equipment
  • B. It can expose energized parts and allow shock or electrocution
  • C. On site, a dry wooden barricade placed to keep workers away
  • D. A cord inspected and found undamaged before use

Damaged insulation can remove the barrier between workers and electricity. This is the correct answer.

Which statement about electrical hazard recognition is correct?
  • A. At the worksite, a safety sign that warns workers about energized equipment
  • B. Even lower voltage can injure or kill under the wrong conditions
  • C. At the worksite, a dry wooden barricade placed to keep workers away
  • D. On the project, a cord inspected and found undamaged before use

Electrical injury depends on current path, conditions, and exposure. This is the correct answer.

On site, why is damaged insulation on a cord dangerous?
  • A. In this case, a safety sign that warns workers about energized equipment
  • B. On site, it can expose energized parts and allow shock or electrocution
  • C. In this case, a dry wooden barricade placed to keep workers away
  • D. During work, a cord inspected and found undamaged before use

Damaged insulation can remove the barrier between workers and electricity. This is the correct answer.

A crew is reviewing Focus Four electrocution hazards before starting work. Which situation should be treated as an electrical hazard?
  • A. During the shift, a dry wooden barricade placed to keep workers away
  • B. On site, a cord inspected and found undamaged before use
  • C. Water collecting around temporary electrical equipment
  • D. On site, a properly stored nonconductive tool

Water can increase the chance of electrical shock when electrical equipment is present. This is the correct answer.

Which condition requires attention before work continues?
  • A. In the field, a dry wooden barricade placed to keep workers away
  • B. At the worksite, a cord inspected and found undamaged before use
  • C. A burning smell or heat from electrical equipment
  • D. At the worksite, a properly stored nonconductive tool

Heat or odor may indicate electrical failure or overload. This is the correct answer.

What is a warning sign of unsafe electrical work?
  • A. During the shift, a cord inspected and found undamaged before use
  • B. During the shift, a properly stored nonconductive tool
  • C. During the shift, a safety sign that warns workers about energized equipment
  • D. A panel cover is missing and energized parts are accessible

Missing covers can expose workers to energized parts. This is the correct answer.

Key Terms in This Domain

  • Approach distance: Minimum safe distance from energized power lines for unqualified workers and equipment
  • Qualified person (electrical): Worker trained in construction or operation of electric equipment and the hazards involved
  • Hand protection: Gloves selected for the specific hazard: cut, chemical, electrical, abrasion, or thermal
  • Housekeeping: Keeping work areas free of debris, scrap, and tripping hazards, first line of defense for slips, trips, and fires
  • OSH Act: The 1970 federal law that created OSHA and established worker safety rights and employer duties
  • General duty clause: OSH Act Section 5(a)(1): employer must furnish a workplace free from recognized hazards likely to cause death or serious physical harm
  • OSHA complaint: A worker may file a confidential complaint requesting an OSHA inspection of a hazardous workplace
  • Whistleblower protection: Section 11(c) prohibits retaliation against workers who exercise OSHA-protected rights
  • Safety net system: Net installed below a work surface to catch workers who fall
  • Anchorage: Secure point capable of supporting at least 5,000 lbs per worker, or designed by a qualified person with a 2:1 safety factor
