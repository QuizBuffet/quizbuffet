How Hard Is the CompTIA Security+ Exam? A 6-Week Plan

What makes Security+ SY0-701 hard, what the passing score really means, and a six-week plan that uses free practice questions to close the gaps.

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Security+ is the most common first security certification, and the honest answer to how hard it is: it is broad rather than deep. Nothing on the SY0-701 exam is obscure, but it covers five domains, up to 90 questions in 90 minutes, and a handful of performance-based items that ask you to do something rather than recall something. Most people who fail did not run out of knowledge. They ran out of time or misread scenario questions.

What the numbers mean

The passing score is 750 on a scale of 100 to 900. That is not 83 percent. CompTIA scales the raw score, so the practical target is answering roughly three quarters of the questions correctly with no domain left blank. The domains are weighted: Security Operations is 28 percent, Threats and Vulnerabilities 22 percent, Security Program Management 20 percent, Security Architecture 18 percent, and General Security Concepts 12 percent. Your study time should follow those weights.

Why people find it hard

  • Scenario wording. Questions describe a situation and ask for the best next step, not a definition. Two answers are usually defensible and one is best.
  • Performance-based questions. Expect a few drag-and-drop or simulation items early in the exam. Skip them, answer the multiple choice, then come back.
  • Acronyms. The exam assumes you can read an acronym without a gloss. If you cannot expand it on sight, you cannot answer the question.

A six-week plan

  1. Week 1. Read the official SY0-701 objectives and write every sub-objective down. Take 20 questions in each domain on QuizBuffet without studying first, to see where you actually stand.
  2. Weeks 2 and 3. Work the two heaviest domains, Security Operations and Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations. Read the explanation for every wrong answer, including the distractors.
  3. Week 4. Security Architecture and Security Program Management. These are the domains people skip and then lose points on.
  4. Week 5. General Security Concepts, then mixed quizzes across all domains until you hold 80 percent or better.
  5. Week 6. Timed sessions of 90 questions. Practice skipping performance-based items and returning to them. Review only what you miss.

How long people actually study

With some IT background, six to eight weeks of an hour a day is typical. With no background, plan on twelve, and consider Network+ first, since Security+ assumes you know how networks work. Either way, practice questions are the fastest way to find out what you do not know, and every one here is free.
