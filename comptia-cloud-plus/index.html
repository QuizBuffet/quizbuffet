Exam structure and difficulty

The CompTIA Cloud+ exam is organized into 6 domains, each weighted by the official CompTIA exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Cloud Architecture at 23% of the exam — that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is DevOps Fundamentals at 10%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: Maximum 90 questions · 90 minutes · Passing score 750/900 · Multiple-choice and performance-based. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss — many over-study lower-weighted material because it feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill CV0-004 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios — the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting — you can fail the whole exam by neglecting Cloud Architecture. (3) Not timing practice sessions — the exam has a real clock, and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.
