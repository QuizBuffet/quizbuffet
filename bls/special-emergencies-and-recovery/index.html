8.0 Special Emergencies and Recovery — BLS Practice Quiz

24 exam-style questions covering 8% of the BLS exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the Basic Life Support (BLS) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

An adult is unresponsive with slow shallow breathing after a suspected drug exposure, and naloxone is available. AHA Part 9 makes a key safety point. Which statement matches?

Answer: Naloxone is safe to administer even if you're not sure what drug the person took

AHA Part 9 explicitly says naloxone is safe to administer even if you are not sure what drug the person took.

AHA Part 9 describes naloxone handheld autoinjectors. How does the manual characterize their action?

Answer: Naloxone autoinjectors deliver a single dose via an intramuscular injection

AHA Part 9 says naloxone handheld autoinjectors deliver a single dose via an intramuscular injection.

AHA Part 10 describes the typical type of chest discomfort in a heart attack. Which description matches?

Answer: Discomfort that may feel like squeezing, pressure, fullness, or pain in the chest

AHA Part 10 describes heart attack chest discomfort as possibly feeling like squeezing, pressure, fullness, or pain in the center or left side of the chest.
