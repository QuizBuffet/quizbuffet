Sample Questions

What does scanning scope define in a cloud vulnerability management program?

Answer: The boundaries of what is included in vulnerability scans — which cloud resources, networks, applications, and environments are assessed for vulnerabilities

Scanning scope establishes the boundaries of vulnerability assessment — which assets, environments, IP ranges, applications, and cloud services are included or excluded from scans. Without defined scope, critical assets may be missed or scans may violate boundaries. This is the correct answer.

What does vulnerability identification involve in a cloud security program?

Answer: Discovering and cataloging security weaknesses in cloud resources — including misconfigured services, unpatched software, weak credentials, and insecure network configurations — through automated scanning, manual review, and threat intelligence

Vulnerability identification discovers and catalogs security weaknesses — misconfigurations, outdated software, weak authentication, exposed services — creating the inventory of issues that must be assessed and remediated. This is the correct answer.

What does vulnerability assessment involve after vulnerabilities have been identified?

Answer: Evaluating each identified vulnerability to determine its severity, exploitability, business impact, and risk level — enabling prioritized remediation decisions based on risk rather than volume

Assessment evaluates each vulnerability's severity (CVSS score), exploitability (public exploits available?), business impact (what data or services are affected?), and contextual risk (is this internet-facing?) — producing a prioritized remediation list that directs limited security resources to the most impactful threats first. This is the correct answer.
