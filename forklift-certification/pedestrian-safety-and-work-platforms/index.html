Sample Questions

Why are forklifts around pedestrians a serious concern?

Answer: Pedestrians can be struck, crushed, or pinned by moving forklifts or loads

The workbook notes that many forklift-related incidents involve pedestrians, so separation and awareness are critical. This is the correct answer.

Answer: Minimize blind spots, highlight intersections, and use caution before entering

The workbook recommends minimizing blind spots and highlighting intersections and restricted areas. This is the correct answer.
