About this certification

The CPA license is the most respected credential in U.S. accounting, required for signing audit opinions and a strong differentiator for tax, advisory, and finance roles. Under the CPA Evolution model (2024+), candidates take three Core sections (Auditing and Attestation — AUD, Financial Accounting and Reporting — FAR, Taxation and Regulation — REG) plus one Discipline section of their choice (Business Analysis and Reporting — BAR, Information Systems and Controls — ISC, or Tax Compliance and Planning — TCP). Each section is 4 hours, with multiple-choice questions and task-based simulations. Licensing also requires 150 college credit hours and 1–2 years of supervised experience, administered state-by-state.
