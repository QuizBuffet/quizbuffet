Why FC0-U61 matters in 2026

CompTIA ITF+ is one of the most recognized credentials issued by CompTIA, and 2026 hiring data continues to show FC0-U61 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 6 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Infrastructure. For candidates competing in the CompTIA ecosystem, FC0-U61 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take FC0-U61

FC0-U61 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the CompTIA ecosystem. In short: IT fundamentals for beginners and career changers. If you are early in your career, FC0-U61 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The CompTIA ITF+ exam is organized into 6 domains, each weighted by the official CompTIA exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Infrastructure at 22% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Database Fundamentals at 11%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 75 questions · 60 minutes · Passing score 650/900 · Multiple-choice. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill FC0-U61 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Infrastructure. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

FC0-U61 questions, answered

How many questions are on the ITF+ exam?

A maximum of 75 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes.

What is the passing score for ITF+?

You need 650 out of 900.

How much does CompTIA ITF+ cost?

CompTIA's standard exam price is currently around $130 to $150 USD, notably cheaper than CompTIA's professional-level certifications since ITF+ is a pre-professional exam. Check CompTIA's official store for the current rate.

Do I need ITF+ before A+?

No, it is not a prerequisite for A+ or any other CompTIA certification. It exists as an optional on-ramp for complete beginners or students deciding whether IT is the right career path.

Does ITF+ expire?

No. ITF+ does not expire and requires no renewal, unlike CompTIA's professional-level certifications (A+, Network+, Security+, and others), which are valid for 3 years.
