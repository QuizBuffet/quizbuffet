Why DA0-002 matters in 2026

CompTIA Data+ is one of the most recognized credentials issued by CompTIA, and 2026 hiring data continues to show DA0-002 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 5 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Data Analysis. For candidates competing in the CompTIA ecosystem, DA0-002 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take DA0-002

DA0-002 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the CompTIA ecosystem. In short: Data acquisition, analysis, and governance for data-driven decision making. If you are early in your career, DA0-002 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. Exam structure and difficulty

The CompTIA Data+ exam is organized into 5 domains, each weighted by the official CompTIA exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Data Analysis at 24% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Data Governance at 14%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: Maximum 90 questions · 90 minutes · Passing score 720/900 · Multiple-choice and performance-based · Recommended 1.5-2 years of experience with databases, analytical tools, statistics, and data visualization. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill DA0-002 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Data Analysis. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

DA0-002 questions, answered

How many questions are on the Data+ exam?

Up to 90 questions, multiple-choice and performance-based, in 90 minutes.

What is the passing score for Data+?

You need 720 out of 900.

How much does CompTIA Data+ cost?

CompTIA's standard exam price is currently around $400 USD. Check CompTIA's official store for the current rate.

Is Data+ a good certification for beginners?

It works well for people with some hands-on exposure to spreadsheets, basic SQL, or BI tools who want a vendor-neutral credential proving data literacy, but CompTIA recommends 1.5 to 2 years of relevant experience rather than treating it as a complete beginner exam.

Data+ vs a data analytics bootcamp certificate, which is better?

Data+ is a proctored, third-party-validated exam that carries more weight as a resume credential; a bootcamp certificate usually goes deeper into a specific tool stack (like Python or Tableau) but is self-graded and vendor-specific to that program.

How long is Data+ certification valid?

Data+ is valid for 3 years and renews through CompTIA's Continuing Education program.
