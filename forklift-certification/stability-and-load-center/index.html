Sample Questions

Forklift stability is based on what basic principle?

Answer: Two weights balanced on opposite sides of a fulcrum point

Forklift stability is based on the same balancing principle as a teeter-totter, with the front wheels acting as the fulcrum point. This is the correct answer.

Which action increases forward tip-over risk?

Answer: Traveling down a ramp with the load forward when that orientation creates instability

Ramp direction and load position affect the combined center of gravity and forward stability. This is the correct answer.

An operator is preparing to move a load. Why should an operator drive as far under the load as possible before lifting?

Answer: On site, to help keep the load close and reduce forward leverage

Keeping the load close helps control leverage around the front-wheel fulcrum. This is the correct answer.
