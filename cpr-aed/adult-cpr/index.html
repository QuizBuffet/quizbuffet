3.0 Adult CPR — CPR/AED Practice Quiz

65 exam-style questions covering 22% of the CPR/AED exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the CPR / AED Certification practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

Where should an adult receiving CPR be positioned?

Answer: Faceup on a firm, flat surface

Effective adult chest compressions require the person to be faceup on a firm, flat surface. This is the correct answer.

Which alternate technique may help a rescuer with joint problems?

Answer: Place one hand on the sternum and use the other hand to support the wrist

The manual shows an alternate adult compression technique supporting the wrist for rescuers with joint issues. This is the correct answer.

A rescuer is performing adult CPR. Which hand position is acceptable for adult compressions?

Answer: One hand on the lower half of the sternum with the other hand on top (per AHA technique)

Adult CPR commonly uses two hands centered on the lower half of the sternum. This is the correct answer.
