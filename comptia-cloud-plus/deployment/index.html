Sample Questions

Which of the following BEST describes a public cloud deployment model?

Answer: Cloud infrastructure owned and operated by a third-party provider, shared among multiple customers over the internet, with resources provisioned on demand

Public cloud is infrastructure owned and operated by providers like AWS, Azure, or GCP — shared among multiple customers (multi-tenancy) and accessed over the internet with resources available on demand. This is the correct answer.

What distinguishes a private cloud from a public cloud?

Answer: A private cloud is cloud infrastructure dedicated exclusively to a single organization — not shared with other tenants — providing greater control, customization, and isolation

Private cloud is defined by its single-tenant, dedicated nature — all infrastructure resources are reserved for and controlled by one organization, providing complete isolation from other customers. This is the correct answer.

What does on-premises infrastructure refer to in a cloud context?

Answer: Computing infrastructure physically located and operated within an organization's own facilities — managed and maintained by the organization's own IT staff

On-premises (on-prem) refers to computing infrastructure physically located in the organization's own data center or office facilities — purchased, owned, and operated by the organization's IT team. This is the correct answer.
