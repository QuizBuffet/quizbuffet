7.0 Special Situations and Recovery — CPR/AED Practice Quiz

30 exam-style questions covering 10% of the CPR/AED exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the CPR / AED Certification practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

An adult is unresponsive with slow, shallow breathing after a suspected drug exposure, and naloxone is available. AHA Part 9 makes a key safety point about giving naloxone in this situation. Which statement matches?

Answer: Naloxone is safe to administer even if you're not sure what drug the person took

AHA Part 9 explicitly says naloxone is safe to administer even if you are not sure what drug the person took.

Per AHA Part 9, naloxone may be administered by which routes during an opioid emergency response by appropriately trained rescuers?

Answer: Intravenously, intramuscularly, and intranasally

AHA Part 9 lists intravenous, intramuscular, and intranasal as common routes for naloxone administration.

AHA Part 9 describes naloxone autoinjectors as a delivery method for opioid reversal. How does the manual characterize their action?

Answer: Naloxone handheld autoinjectors deliver a single dose via an intramuscular injection

AHA Part 9 says naloxone handheld autoinjectors deliver a single dose via an intramuscular injection.
