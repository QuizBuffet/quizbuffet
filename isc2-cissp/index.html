The CISSP Guide

Everything you need to know before sitting for Certified Information Systems Security Professional

Why CISSP matters in 2026

Certified Information Systems Security Professional is one of the most recognized credentials issued by ISC2, and 2026 hiring data continues to show CISSP on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 8 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Security and Risk Management. For candidates competing in the ISC2 ecosystem, CISSP is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take CISSP

CISSP is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the ISC2 ecosystem. In short: The gold-standard senior cybersecurity certification. If you are early in your career, CISSP is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The Certified Information Systems Security Professional exam is organized into 8 domains, each weighted by the official ISC2 exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Security and Risk Management at 16% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Software Development Security at 10%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 125-150 adaptive items · 3 hours · Multiple-choice and advanced innovative items · Pass score 700/1000. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

How to prepare

A practical four-step plan for CISSP:

  1. Read the official ISC2 exam guide for CISSP and write down every sub-objective. This becomes your study checklist.
  2. Use the domain practice quizzes on this page in weight order (heaviest first). Aim for 80% on each domain before moving on.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill CISSP candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Security and Risk Management. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

CISSP questions, answered

How hard is the CISSP?

It has a reputation as one of the toughest cybersecurity exams, less because any single question is obscure and more because it demands broad, managerial-level fluency across all eight domains at once, from risk governance to cryptography to secure software development. Most candidates study for two to four months.

How many questions are on the CISSP exam?

The English CAT (computerized adaptive testing) exam runs 100 to 150 questions in up to 3 hours; the exact count depends on how the adaptive algorithm scores your answers as you go.

What is the CISSP passing score?

You need 700 out of 1000 on the scaled score. CAT scoring means the exam can end as soon as it is statistically confident you are above or below that line.

How much does the CISSP cost?

The exam fee is $749 USD through Pearson VUE, plus the ISC2 annual maintenance fee once you are certified.

Do you need 5 years of experience for CISSP?

Yes, ISC2 requires 5 years of cumulative, paid work experience in 2 or more of the 8 CISSP domains (4 years if you hold a qualifying 4-year degree or an approved credential). Without enough experience yet, you can still pass the exam and hold the Associate of ISC2 designation until you qualify.

CISSP vs Security+, what is the difference?

Security+ is an entry-level exam covering foundational security concepts. CISSP is a senior-level credential covering the same ground in far more depth plus governance, risk management, and secure architecture, and it requires years of real experience to fully certify. Most people take Security+ years before attempting CISSP.

How long is CISSP valid?

CISSP certification is valid for 3 years, maintained by earning continuing professional education (CPE) credits and paying the annual maintenance fee.

Is a free CISSP practice test enough to prepare?

Practice questions are one part of a broader study plan, useful for finding weak domains and getting comfortable with how CISSP phrases scenario-based questions, but most candidates pair them with the official ISC2 study guide or a structured course given the breadth of the exam.
