2.0 Operations Over People — Part 107 Practice Quiz

Sample Questions

A remote pilot wants to fly an sUAS over a group of non-participant bystanders at an outdoor event without obtaining a waiver. The pilot believes the aircraft may qualify as Category 1. Under 14 CFR Part 107, what maximum weight must the sUAS meet to qualify for a Category 1 operation over people?

Answer: The sUAS must weigh 0.55 pounds or less, including everything onboard at the time of operation

Under 14 CFR 107.115, a Category 1 operation is authorized when the sUAS weighs 0.55 pounds or less, including everything onboard at the time of the operation.

A remote pilot holds a Declaration of Compliance confirming the sUAS meets Category 3 requirements. The pilot is asked to fly over a crowd attending an outdoor music festival. Under 14 CFR Part 107, may a Category 3 sUAS conduct this operation over the open-air assembly?

Answer: No, Category 3 operations may not be conducted over an open-air assembly of human beings under any circumstance

Under 14 CFR 107.125, a Category 3 operation may not be conducted over an open-air assembly of human beings; this prohibition applies regardless of speed, altitude, or the presence of visual observers.

A production company wants to film a car chase scene by flying an sUAS over the moving vehicles on a closed course that is not a controlled or restricted-access site and is not an open-air assembly area. Under Part 107, which sUAS categories may conduct sustained flight over the moving vehicles in this scenario?

Answer: Category 1 and Category 2 sUAS, which may conduct sustained flight over moving vehicles in areas that are not controlled/restricted-access sites and not open-air assemblies

Under 14 CFR 107.145, Category 1 and Category 2 sUAS may conduct sustained flight over moving vehicles in an area that is not a controlled or restricted-access site and not an open-air assembly.
