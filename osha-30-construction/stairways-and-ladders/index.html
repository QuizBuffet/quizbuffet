Sample Questions

Which condition is a common stairway hazard on a construction site?

Answer: Loose debris or materials stored on stair treads

Debris on stairs can cause trips, slips, and falls during access between levels. This is the correct answer.

On site, which condition is a common stairway hazard on a construction site?

Answer: On site, loose debris or materials stored on stair treads

Debris on stairs can cause trips, slips, and falls during access between levels. This is the correct answer.

A supervisor is reviewing safe access with a crew. What should be considered when selecting a ladder?

Answer: The task, height, load rating, environment, and ladder type needed for safe access

Ladder selection should match the work, height, load, and conditions. This is the correct answer.
