2.0 Client Relations and Behavioral Coaching — NASM-CPT Practice Quiz

100 exam-style questions covering 11% of the NASM-CPT exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the Certified Personal Trainer (NASM-CPT) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

A personal trainer is studying the field that focuses on understanding why people participate in sports and exercise. According to NASM, what is this subtopic of psychology called?

Answer: Sport and exercise psychology

NASM defines sport and exercise psychology as a subtopic of psychology that focuses on understanding why people participate in sports and exercise.

A client says they want to lose weight to look better in photos for an upcoming wedding. According to NASM, this type of motivation is best classified as which of the following?

Answer: Extrinsic motivation

NASM defines extrinsic motivation as doing something for rewards or recognition; improving appearance for a public event is extrinsic.

A client says they exercise because the activity itself relieves their stress and energizes them. According to NASM, this is an example of which type of motivation?

Answer: Intrinsic motivation driven by internal satisfaction

NASM defines intrinsic motivation as motivation that comes from within an individual — stress relief and energy are examples.
