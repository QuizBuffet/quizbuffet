2.0 Navigation & Settings — QuickBooks ProAdvisor Practice Quiz

24 exam-style questions covering 12% of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor (Online Certification) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

The gear icon located in the upper right-hand corner of QuickBooks Online provides access to key management areas of the software. It is organized into four named sections for easy navigation. Which of the following correctly lists the four sections found under the QBO gear icon?

Answer: Your Company, Lists, Tools, and Profile

The gear icon contains exactly four sections: Your Company, Lists, Tools, and Profile, as described in the Student Guide.

To access QuickBooks Online from any device, users must navigate to the correct web address and enter their credentials. The Student Guide specifies the URL used to reach the QBO login page. Which URL is used to log in to QuickBooks Online?

Answer: qbo.intuit.com

The Student Guide states that users access QuickBooks Online by going to qbo.intuit.com and entering their user ID and password.

Clicking the +New button at the top of the left navigation bar in QBO reveals a list of transaction screens the user can open. These transaction types are organized into four named groups. Which groups are used to organize the transactions available under +New?

Answer: Customers, Vendors, Employees, and Other

The Student Guide states that +New transactions are grouped by Customers, Vendors, Employees, and Other.
