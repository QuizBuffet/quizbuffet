Sample Questions

What is the main purpose of an effective construction safety and health program?

Answer: To prevent injuries, illnesses, and fatalities by systematically finding and controlling hazards

An effective program helps employers and workers identify hazards, control exposures, and prevent harm before incidents occur. This is the correct answer.
