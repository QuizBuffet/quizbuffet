Why AIP-C01 matters in 2026

AWS Certified Generative AI Developer Professional is one of the most recognized credentials issued by AWS, and 2026 hiring data continues to show AIP-C01 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 5 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Foundation Model Integration, Data Management, and Compliance. For candidates competing in the AWS ecosystem, AIP-C01 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take AIP-C01

AIP-C01 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the AWS ecosystem. In short: Build, integrate, and operate production GenAI solutions on AWS. If you are early in your career, AIP-C01 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill AIP-C01 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Foundation Model Integration, Data Management, and Compliance. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

AIP-C01 questions, answered

How many questions are on the AIP-C01 exam?

75 questions total (65 scored, 10 unscored), multiple-choice and multiple-response, in 180 minutes.

What is the passing score for GenAI Developer Professional?

You need 750 out of 1000.

How much does AWS GenAI Developer Professional cost?

It's AWS's professional-tier exam, $300 USD.

Do you need AI Practitioner before GenAI Developer Professional?

Not required by AWS, but it's a natural first step if you're newer to AI/ML on AWS. This exam assumes hands-on developer experience integrating foundation models, RAG, and agentic AI into real applications, not just conceptual AI knowledge.

How is this different from ML Engineer Associate?

ML Engineer Associate covers building and deploying traditional ML models and pipelines. GenAI Developer Professional focuses specifically on generative AI: integrating foundation models, prompt engineering, RAG architectures, and agentic workflows into production applications.

How long is GenAI Developer Professional valid?

3 years, renewed by retaking the exam or passing a higher-level AWS exam.
