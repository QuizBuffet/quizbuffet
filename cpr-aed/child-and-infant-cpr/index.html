4.0 Child and Infant CPR — CPR/AED Practice Quiz

54 exam-style questions covering 18% of the CPR/AED exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the CPR / AED Certification practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

An instructor reviews the AHA age definitions used in the BLS pediatric section. Which range correctly describes an 'infant' for BLS purposes, excluding the newly born?

Answer: Younger than 1 year

AHA Part 1 defines an infant as someone younger than 1 year, excluding newly born infants in the delivery room.

Which range does AHA Part 1 use to define a 'child' for BLS purposes?

Answer: From 1 year of age to puberty

AHA Part 1 defines a child as anyone from 1 year of age to the onset of puberty.

An infant lies motionless on a kitchen floor. According to Step 1 of the pediatric BLS single-rescuer sequence, what is the rescuer's very first action?

Answer: Verify that the scene is safe for the rescuer and the infant

AHA Part 6 makes Step 1 of the pediatric sequence an explicit scene safety verification for the rescuer and the infant.
