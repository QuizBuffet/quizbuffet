The DEA-C01 Guide

Everything you need to know before sitting for AWS Certified Data Engineer Associate

Why DEA-C01 matters in 2026

AWS Certified Data Engineer Associate is one of the most recognized credentials issued by AWS, and 2026 hiring data continues to show DEA-C01 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 4 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Data Ingestion and Transformation. For candidates competing in the AWS ecosystem, DEA-C01 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take DEA-C01

DEA-C01 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the AWS ecosystem. In short: Implement, monitor, and optimize data pipelines on AWS. If you are early in your career, DEA-C01 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The AWS Certified Data Engineer Associate exam is organized into 4 domains, each weighted by the official AWS exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Data Ingestion and Transformation at 34% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Data Security and Governance at 18%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: 65 questions (50 scored + 15 unscored) · 130 minutes · Multiple-choice and multiple-response · Pass score 720/1000. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill DEA-C01 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Data Ingestion and Transformation. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.

DEA-C01 questions, answered

How many questions are on the DEA-C01 exam?

65 questions total (50 scored, 15 unscored), multiple-choice and multiple-response, in 130 minutes.

What is the passing score for Data Engineer Associate?

You need 720 out of 1000.

How much does AWS Data Engineer Associate cost?

It's AWS's associate-tier exam, $150 USD.

Data Engineer Associate vs Data Analytics Specialty, what changed?

Data Engineer Associate (DEA-C01) replaced the retired Data Analytics Specialty exam, moving data pipeline and analytics content into the standard associate tier with expanded coverage of ingestion, orchestration, and data governance.

Do you need coding experience for Data Engineer Associate?

Yes, some. AWS expects familiarity with SQL and at least one general-purpose language (Python is common) plus hands-on experience with core AWS data services like Glue, Redshift, and Kinesis.

How long is Data Engineer Associate valid?

3 years, renewed by retaking the exam or passing a higher-level AWS exam.
