2.0 Client Relations and Behavioral Coaching NASM-CPT Practice Quiz

100 exam-style questions covering 11% of the NASM-CPT exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the Certified Personal Trainer (NASM-CPT) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

A personal trainer is studying the field that focuses on understanding why people participate in sports and exercise. According to NASM, what is this subtopic of psychology called?
  • A. Sport and exercise psychology
  • B. Behavioral kinesiology
  • C. Performance neuroscience
  • D. Cognitive movement theory

NASM defines sport and exercise psychology as a subtopic of psychology that focuses on understanding why people participate in sports and exercise.

A client says they want to lose weight to look better in photos for an upcoming wedding. According to NASM, this type of motivation is best classified as which of the following?
  • A. Intrinsic motivation
  • B. Extrinsic motivation
  • C. Self-determined motivation
  • D. Cognitive motivation

NASM defines extrinsic motivation as doing something for rewards or recognition; improving appearance for a public event is extrinsic.

A new fitness professional asks what kinds of questions sport and exercise psychology helps answer. According to NASM, sport and exercise psychology focuses on understanding which two specific aspects of physical activity?
  • A. Genetic predispositions and metabolic responses to training stimuli
  • B. Motives and barriers to participation in sports and exercise
  • C. Biomechanical efficiency and kinetic chain function during dynamic movement
  • D. Cardiovascular adaptations and hormonal responses to chronic training

NASM defines sport and exercise psychology as focusing on understanding why people participate in sports and exercise, including motives and barriers to participation.

A personal trainer reviews the difference between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation with a client. According to NASM, which of the following is an example of extrinsic motivation for exercise?
  • A. Exercising because it provides stress relief and improves mood
  • B. Exercising to find new ways to be challenged physically
  • C. Receiving social recognition or rewards from competitions for completing a goal
  • D. Exercising because it generates an internal sense of energy and well-being

NASM identifies social recognition, rewards from competitions, and improvement of physical appearance as examples of extrinsic motivation.

A personal trainer is helping a client identify motivators that may keep them exercising long term. According to NASM, why is intrinsic motivation particularly valuable in exercise programming?
  • A. It allows clients to bypass setting specific goals because it is self-sustaining without structure
  • B. It is the only form of motivation that NASM recognizes as legitimate for exercise
  • C. It enables faster physiological adaptation than extrinsic motivation produces
  • D. Intrinsic motivation is strongly related to long-term adherence to exercise

NASM states that intrinsic motivation is strongly related to long-term adherence, making it particularly valuable for sustainable exercise behavior.

A client asks the trainer whether they need a sport psychologist or a clinical psychologist for their issues. According to NASM, what distinguishes psychologists and psychiatrists from CPTs in terms of professional role?
  • A. Psychologists and psychiatrists primarily develop training programs for elite athletes only
  • B. Psychologists and psychiatrists provide nutritional counseling beyond a CPT's scope of practice
  • C. Psychologists and psychiatrists are trained and licensed professionals who treat people with mental illnesses
  • D. Psychologists and psychiatrists are responsible for prescribing all forms of physical rehabilitation

NASM identifies psychologists and psychiatrists as trained and licensed professionals who treat people with mental illnesses, distinct from a CPT's role.

A client tells their trainer they exercise primarily to win trophies in age-group running competitions. According to NASM, which of the following is a defining feature of extrinsic motivation in this scenario?
  • A. The client is internally driven by personal satisfaction from training itself
  • B. The motivation is sustainable in the long term once it is established with the client
  • C. The motivation is generated by physiological feedback during exercise sessions
  • D. The behavior is driven by external rewards or recognition rather than internal satisfaction

NASM defines extrinsic motivation as occurring when someone does something for rewards or recognition, exactly what trophy-winning represents.

A personal trainer is integrating psychology into their practice while staying within scope. According to NASM, what role does psychology play in the personal training profession?
  • A. Psychology is a niche specialization that only matters for elite athletic populations
  • B. Psychology applies primarily to clients with diagnosed mental health conditions and few others
  • C. Psychology is incidental to fitness training and is mainly used for athlete recovery management
  • D. Psychology is an important component of behavioral change and plays a key role in adopting a regular habit to exercise

NASM states that psychology is an important component to behavioral change and plays a key role in adopting a regular habit to exercise.
