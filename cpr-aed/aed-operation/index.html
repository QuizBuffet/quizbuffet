5.0 AED Operation — CPR/AED Practice Quiz

55 exam-style questions covering 18% of the CPR/AED exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the CPR / AED Certification practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

What is the main purpose of an AED during cardiac arrest?

Answer: To analyze the heart rhythm and deliver a shock when a shockable rhythm is detected

An AED identifies rhythms that may need defibrillation and can deliver a shock when indicated. This is the correct answer.

A rescuer is using an AED on an adult. What is the main purpose of an AED during cardiac arrest?

Answer: During the shift, to analyze the heart rhythm and deliver a shock when a shockable rhythm is detected

An AED identifies rhythms that may need defibrillation and can deliver a shock when indicated. This is the correct answer.

During AED skills practice, where should the AED be placed when it arrives?

Answer: At the person's side near the rescuer operating it

The AED should be positioned where it can be operated while CPR continues as much as possible. This is the correct answer.
