Sample Questions

The barber chair's headrest MUST be disinfected and covered before each client because

Answer: it directly contacts the client's neck and head, making it a surface that must be prepared to prevent cross-contamination

The barber chair headrest contacts the client's neck, scalp, and potentially hair and skin during every seated service; as a surface that touches multiple clients throughout the day, it must be disinfected with an EPA-registered product between clients and then covered with a clean head-rest paper or fresh protective covering to present a sanitary surface for each new client and prevent cross-contamination.

The straight razor used in a barbershop must be

Answer: a single-use disposable blade or, if reusable, must be properly cleaned and disinfected between each client

Straight razors used in barbershop services contact the client's skin directly and may produce minor cuts and bleeding; for infection control, disposable single-use razor blades are the professional standard because they eliminate the risk of cross-contamination between clients entirely; if reusable straight razors (with replaceable blades) are used, the blade must be changed for each client, and the handle must be thoroughly cleaned and disinfected with an EPA-registered disinfectant between clients.

A neck strip (neck paper) is used in barbering to

Answer: protect the client's clothing and prevent the cape from touching the client's skin directly

A neck strip (neck paper or tissue) is a disposable paper band placed around the client's neck before the barber cape is fastened; it serves as a sanitary barrier between the reusable cape and the client's skin — preventing the cape from directly contacting the client's neck; since capes cannot be laundered between every client, the single-use neck strip ensures each client has a clean, fresh barrier against their skin; it also catches loose hair clippings that might otherwise fall inside the cape.
