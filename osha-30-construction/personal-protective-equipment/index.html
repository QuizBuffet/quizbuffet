Sample Questions

What is the main role of PPE in construction safety?
  • A. To protect workers from hazards that remain after other controls are applied
  • B. To remove the need for hazard identification
  • C. To replace guardrails, ventilation, and safe work practices in every case
  • D. To make unsafe work acceptable without planning

PPE helps reduce exposure when hazards cannot be fully eliminated or controlled by other methods. This is the correct answer.

On site, what is the main role of PPE in construction safety?
  • A. On site, to protect workers from hazards that remain after other controls are applied
  • B. On site, to replace guardrails, ventilation, and safe work practices in every case
  • C. On site, to make unsafe work acceptable without planning
  • D. On site, to remove the need for hazard identification

PPE helps reduce exposure when hazards cannot be fully eliminated or controlled by other methods. This is the correct answer.

Which statement best describes a limitation of PPE?
  • A. PPE works even when damaged or worn incorrectly
  • B. PPE depends on correct selection, fit, condition, and use
  • C. PPE removes the need for worker training
  • D. PPE eliminates every construction hazard at the source

PPE protection can fail when it is poorly selected, damaged, improperly fitted, or not used correctly. This is the correct answer.

Which action best addresses PPE role and limitations? Which statement best describes a limitation of PPE?
  • A. On site, PPE removes the need for worker training
  • B. On site, PPE depends on correct selection, fit, condition, and use
  • C. On site, PPE eliminates every construction hazard at the source
  • D. On site, PPE works even when damaged or worn incorrectly

PPE protection can fail when it is poorly selected, damaged, improperly fitted, or not used correctly. This is the correct answer.

When is eye or face protection needed?
  • A. Solely when the worker personally wants it
  • B. When workers are exposed to flying particles, chemical splash, sparks, or similar hazards
  • C. Solely after debris has already struck someone
  • D. Solely for office work inside the trailer

Eye and face protection should match the task hazards such as impact, splash, or sparks. This is the correct answer.

During PPE planning for a construction task, A task exposes workers to flying debris from cutting. What should the supervisor consider?
  • A. Ignore debris if the task takes a short time
  • B. Use sunglasses as the main protection for all cutting tasks
  • C. Use guards or barriers where feasible and provide appropriate eye or face protection
  • D. Let nearby workers decide whether protection is comfortable

PPE should be combined with other controls such as guards and barriers when flying debris is present. This is the correct answer.

A task exposes workers to flying debris from cutting. What should the supervisor consider?
  • A. On site, use sunglasses as the main protection for all cutting tasks
  • B. On site, let nearby workers decide whether protection is comfortable
  • C. On site, use guards or barriers where feasible and provide appropriate eye or face protection
  • D. On site, ignore debris if the task takes a short time

PPE should be combined with other controls such as guards and barriers when flying debris is present. This is the correct answer.

Why is PPE considered a last line of defense?
  • A. It is never useful on construction sites
  • B. It works without inspection or training
  • C. It should be used only after a worker is injured
  • D. It protects the worker but usually does not remove the hazard itself

PPE reduces exposure at the worker, while other controls may eliminate or isolate the hazard. This is the correct answer.

Key Terms in This Domain

  • Hard hat (Class E/G/C): Head protection; Class E rated for high-voltage, Class G for general, Class C provides no electrical protection
  • High-visibility apparel: ANSI/ISEA 107 garments worn around traffic and equipment to make workers visible
  • Respiratory protection program: 29 CFR 1910.134: written program with selection, fit testing, training, medical evaluation, and recordkeeping
  • Assured equipment grounding conductor program (AEGCP): Documented inspection program permitted as an alternative to GFCI on construction sites
  • Eye and face protection: Safety glasses, goggles, or face shields meeting ANSI Z87.1 against impact, chemical splash, or radiation
  • Hearing protection: Earplugs or earmuffs required when noise exposure equals or exceeds 85 dBA TWA
  • Hand protection: Gloves selected for the specific hazard: cut, chemical, electrical, abrasion, or thermal
  • Foot protection: Safety-toe footwear meeting ASTM F2413 against impact, compression, and puncture
  • Traffic control (work zones): MUTCD Part 6: temporary traffic control plans, flaggers, channelizers, and high-visibility apparel
  • OSHA complaint: A worker may file a confidential complaint requesting an OSHA inspection of a hazardous workplace
