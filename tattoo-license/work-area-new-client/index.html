Sample Questions

When setting up supplies for a new client after breakdown, the candidate should

Answer: begin with freshly disinfected or newly barriered surfaces and new sterile packaged implements from the supply kit — never reusing single-session items from the previous client's procedure

Each new client requires a completely fresh setup: all surfaces must have been disinfected during the breakdown of the previous procedure and new barriers applied before the new client's supplies are arranged; new sterile packaged implements (needles, tubes) from the supply kit are used for each client; no materials from the previous client's procedure — used or unused — are carried forward to the next client's session.

During Section 6, the examiner instructs the candidate to demonstrate disinfection and storage for transportation of the foot switch. The foot switch must first be

Answer: wiped thoroughly with EPA-registered disinfectant wipes on all surfaces before being placed on the floor under the table as directed by the examiner

The NIC CIB specifies in Section 6 that the examiner will instruct the candidate to 'demonstrate disinfection and storage for transportation of foot switch only' and to 'place the foot switch on the floor under the table'; the foot switch must be wiped with EPA-registered disinfectant wipes on all surfaces (particularly the areas contacted during use), allowed to air dry or the contact time to elapse, and then placed on the floor under the table as directed — separated from clean supplies during transport because it is designated as a contaminated item throughout the examination.

When the examiner instructs the candidate to 'follow all steps to ensure personal protection' during Section 6, the candidate demonstrates

Answer: donning fresh medical-grade gloves after performing hand hygiene, confirming readiness to receive the next client safely

After completing the new client work area setup and foot switch procedures, the personal protection conclusion for Section 6 involves the candidate performing hand hygiene and donning fresh gloves — demonstrating they are prepared with appropriate PPE to receive and safely provide services to the next client; this confirms the full infection control cycle: breakdown, new setup, personal protection to be ready for service.
