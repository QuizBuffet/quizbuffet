About QuizBuffet

QuizBuffet (sometimes written Quiz Buffet) is a free practice-test site for professional certifications and state licenses. It covers IT and cybersecurity exams such as CompTIA Security+, A+, Network+, CCNA, and CISSP, cloud exams from AWS and Microsoft Azure, and licensed trades and healthcare credentials such as CPR and AED, BLS, OSHA 10 and 30, forklift certification, CDL Class A, the NMLS SAFE mortgage loan originator test, real estate, cosmetology, barbering, and the CPA exam sections.

Every certification is broken into the same domains the real exam uses, with the same weighting, so the time you spend here mirrors the time you will spend on test day. Each question comes with instant feedback and an explanation for every answer choice, including the wrong ones. You can drill one domain at a time, mix all domains, filter by difficulty, and pick a session length from five questions to the full bank.

There is no account, no email capture, and no paywall. Your progress is saved in your own browser and never leaves your device. Questions are written to match each exam's published objectives and are checked for accuracy, balance across difficulty levels, and coverage of every sub-objective before a certification goes live. If you find an error, use the contact link in the footer and include the question number.
