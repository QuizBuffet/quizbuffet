The N10-009 Guide

Everything you need to know before sitting for CompTIA Network+

Why N10-009 matters in 2026

CompTIA Network+ is one of the most recognized credentials issued by CompTIA, and 2026 hiring data continues to show N10-009 on job postings as either required or strongly preferred for the roles it targets. The certification validates that you can apply real working knowledge, not just recall facts, across 5 distinct exam domains, with the largest weight on Network Troubleshooting. For candidates competing in the CompTIA ecosystem, N10-009 is a clear signal to a hiring manager that you have invested in measurable, third-party-verified competence.

Who should take N10-009

N10-009 is most useful for professionals working in or moving toward the CompTIA ecosystem. In short: Core networking skills for IT infrastructure professionals. If you are early in your career, N10-009 is one of the fastest credentials to add to a résumé that recruiters actively screen for. If you are mid-career, it formalizes the skills you already use day-to-day and unlocks roles that gate on it. If you are switching tracks, it gives you a structured curriculum that tells you exactly what to study, in what order, weighted by what the real exam tests.

Exam structure and difficulty

The CompTIA Network+ exam is organized into 5 domains, each weighted by the official CompTIA exam guide. The heaviest weighting is Network Troubleshooting at 24% of the exam, so that is where you should spend the most preparation time. The lightest is Network Security at 14%, meaning you can dedicate roughly proportional review time without over-investing.

Format details: Maximum 90 questions · 90 minutes · Passing score 720/900 · Multiple-choice and performance-based. Following the domain weights is the single biggest leverage point candidates miss, because many over-study lower-weighted material that feels comfortable.

How to prepare

A practical four-step plan for N10-009:

  1. Read the official CompTIA exam guide for N10-009 and write down every sub-objective. This becomes your study checklist.
  2. Use the domain practice quizzes on this page in weight order (heaviest first). Aim for 80% on each domain before moving on.
  3. When you miss a question, read the explanation for every wrong answer, because that contrastive learning is where understanding compounds.
  4. Once every domain is at 80%+, take the Mix Quiz repeatedly to simulate real exam conditions across all topics.

Career outcomes and salary

Holders of N10-009 in the US currently see compensation in the range of $55k to $95k per year, with median around $74k. Network admin / junior network engineer Salary varies by region, employer size, and complementary skills, but the N10-009 credential consistently lifts the floor of what you can negotiate against.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill N10-009 candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. (2) Skipping the heaviest-weighted domain because it feels less interesting, which can fail the whole exam by neglecting Network Troubleshooting. (3) Not timing practice sessions, when the exam has a real clock and pacing is its own skill. Build timing into your last two weeks of prep.
