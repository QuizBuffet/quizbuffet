Sample Questions

Which are the four dimensions of service management in ITIL 4?

Answer: Organizations and people; information and technology; partners and suppliers; value streams and processes

The four dimensions are organizations and people, information and technology, partners and suppliers, and value streams and processes. This is the correct answer.

Which statement about four dimensions of service management is CORRECT?

Answer: Organizations and people; information and technology; partners and suppliers; value streams and processes

The four dimensions are organizations and people, information and technology, partners and suppliers, and value streams and processes. This is the correct answer.

Which statement about organizations and people is CORRECT?

Answer: Culture and communication can affect how effectively services are managed

People, culture, communication, and structures influence service management outcomes. This is the correct answer.
