Sample Questions

The CDL manual opens the inspection chapter by stating a single most important reason for inspecting a vehicle before every trip. Which reason matches the manual?

Answer: Safety — for the driver and for other road users

The CDL manual states that safety is the most important reason for inspecting a vehicle — safety for yourself and for other road users.

At the end of the CDL manual's inspection chapter, the manual makes a statement about federal and state law regarding unsafe vehicles. Which statement matches the manual?

Answer: Federal and state laws forbid operating an unsafe vehicle

The CDL manual states that federal and state laws forbid operating an unsafe vehicle.

Step 1 of the CDL manual's seven-step inspection is called Vehicle Overview. What does the manual say the driver does in Step 1?

Answer: Review the last vehicle inspection report (DVIR), signing it only if defects were noted and certified repaired

The CDL manual states Step 1 is to review the last vehicle inspection report; the driver must sign the report only if defects were noted and certified to be repaired or not needed to be repaired.
