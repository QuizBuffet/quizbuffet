What best describes Confidentiality concerns in CompTIA ITF+ security terminology?

Answer: The concept of Confidentiality concerns refers to risks that expose information to people or systems that are not authorized to see it; operationally, the task is to focus on preventing unauthorized viewing or disclosure.

What best describes Snooping in CompTIA ITF+ security terminology?

Answer: The concept of Snooping refers to casual or unauthorized looking through data, screens, files, or network information without permission; operationally, the task is to prevent unauthorized viewing.

What best describes Eavesdropping in CompTIA ITF+ security terminology?

Answer: The concept of Eavesdropping refers to secretly listening to communications, such as network traffic, calls, or conversations, to gather information; operationally, the task is to protect communications from being listened to.
