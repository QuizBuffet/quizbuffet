4.0 Chart of Accounts & Products/Services — QuickBooks ProAdvisor Practice Quiz

20 exam-style questions covering 10% of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the QuickBooks ProAdvisor (Online Certification) practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

A new bookkeeper needs to find the Chart of Accounts in QuickBooks Online. They are not sure where to access it. Which of the following is a valid navigation path to the Chart of Accounts in QBO?

Answer: Gear icon > Your Company > Chart of Accounts

Correct — the Chart of Accounts can be accessed via the Gear icon > Your Company > Chart of Accounts, or via the left navigation bar Accounting tab.

When creating a new product or service in QuickBooks Online, a user must select one of four types. Choosing the correct type determines how the item behaves on transactions. Which set of options correctly lists all four product/service types in QBO?

Answer: Inventory, Non-Inventory, Service, Bundle

Correct — the four product/service types in QuickBooks Online are Inventory, Non-Inventory, Service, and Bundle.

A music store sells guitar starter packs that include a guitar, a strap, a tuner, and a case — all sold together as one packaged offering. Which QuickBooks Online product/service type best represents this packaged product on sales forms?

Answer: Bundle — a collection of products and services sold together

Correct — a Bundle is a collection of products and services sold together on a sales form, which fits the guitar starter pack scenario.
