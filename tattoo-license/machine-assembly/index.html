Sample Questions

Before beginning machine assembly, the candidate must present the packaged tube and needle to the examiner to

Answer: demonstrate that the tube and needle are sterile by showing intact, uncompromised original packaging before opening

Presenting the packaged tube and needle to the examiner before opening demonstrates that these critical skin-penetrating implements arrived at the procedure area in their original, intact sterile packaging — confirming they have not been compromised, opened, or contaminated before use; the examiner can verify the packaging is sealed, undamaged, and within its expiration date; this step is explicitly required by the NIC Tattooing Practical Examination before machine assembly begins.

During the NIC Tattooing Practical Examination machine assessment, after being instructed to power the machine on and off, the candidate must

Answer: power the machine on and off without touching the needle to skin at any point

The machine power demonstration assesses whether the candidate has correctly assembled and connected the machine so it functions; the examiner instructs the candidate to power the machine on and off; the CIB explicitly states 'Do NOT touch the needle to skin' during this demonstration — the task is to confirm machine function, not to begin tattooing.

The needle packaging and tube packaging opened during machine assembly must be placed in the

Answer: 'Trash' container — packaging is a single-use soft waste item

Opened sterile packaging (paper-plastic pouches) for needles and tubes are single-use soft waste items; after removing the sterile contents, the empty packaging is discarded in the 'Trash' container; packaging is not a sharp, not a reusable implement, and cannot be returned to the supply kit.
