Sample Questions

What is the purpose of the information security management practice?

Answer: To protect the information needed by the organization to conduct its business

The practice protects organizational information so business can be conducted safely and reliably. This is the correct answer.

Which statement best matches the ITIL 4 purpose or key terms for information security management?

Answer: It helps ensure information risks are understood and treated according to organizational needs

The statement aligns with the purpose and expected terminology for the information security management practice. This is the correct answer.

A federal agency's service desk is reviewing how it handles user requests. Which activity is most closely associated with relationship management?

Answer: It helps maintain stakeholder satisfaction and support continual value co-creation

The activity supports the purpose and key terms of the relationship management practice. This is the correct answer.
