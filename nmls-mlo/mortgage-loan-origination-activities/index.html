Sample Questions

A consumer calls an MLO to ask about general rate ranges and requests product information. The consumer has not yet provided the items needed to constitute an application. Under Regulation Z, this contact is best classified as:

Answer: A pre-application inquiry that does not yet trigger the Loan Estimate clock

Correct — until the consumer provides all six application items, the contact is a pre-application inquiry under 12 CFR 1026.2(a)(3).

MLOs handle nonpublic personal information from consumers. Mishandling this information can violate GLBA and state law. An MLO who collects a consumer's Social Security number, income documents, and bank statements must:

Answer: Safeguard the information consistent with the company's GLBA-required information security program

Correct — GLBA's Safeguards Rule (16 CFR 314) requires an information security program protecting nonpublic personal information.

At application, the consumer receives certain initial disclosures along with the Loan Estimate. Both Regulation Z and Regulation X drive the timing. Which disclosure is generally required within 3 business days of application along with the Loan Estimate on a purchase transaction?

Answer: The CFPB special information booklet (Your Home Loan Toolkit)

Correct — 12 CFR 1026.19(g) requires the special information booklet within 3 business days of application on purchase loans.
