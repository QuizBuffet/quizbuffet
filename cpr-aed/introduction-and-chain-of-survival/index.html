1.0 Introduction and Chain of Survival — CPR/AED Practice Quiz

24 exam-style questions covering 8% of the CPR/AED exam. Instant feedback on every answer, progress tracking, no signup required.

This domain is part of the CPR / AED Certification practice test. Each question is tagged by exam objective and difficulty so you can drill exactly the areas you need.

Sample Questions

A coworker asks why bystander CPR matters when EMS will arrive in a few minutes anyway. Which statement best summarizes the impact of immediate high-quality CPR combined with early defibrillation on survival from cardiac arrest?

Answer: It can double or triple the chances of survival

AHA states that high-quality CPR started immediately after cardiac arrest combined with early defibrillation can double or triple the chances of survival.

An instructor draws the AHA Chain of Survival on the board and asks the class to identify the very first link a rescuer acts on when someone collapses. Which link is it?

Answer: Recognition and emergency activation

Recognition and emergency activation is the first link — the rescuer recognizes the arrest and activates the emergency response system.

After confirming the scene is safe, you reach an adult lying motionless on the floor of a coffee shop. Which technique is the AHA-recommended way to check whether the adult is responsive?

Answer: Tap the shoulders and shout, 'Hey! Are you OK?'

AHA Part 2 instructs rescuers to tap the adult's shoulders and shout, 'Hey! Are you OK?' to check responsiveness.
