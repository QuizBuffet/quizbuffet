Sample Questions

Why was OSHA established?
  • A. To help assure safe and healthful working conditions for workers
  • B. To replace every employer safety program with a federal safety manual
  • C. To approve every construction schedule before work begins
  • D. To guarantee construction work has no remaining hazards

OSHA’s core purpose is to help assure safe and healthful working conditions through standards, enforcement, training, outreach, education, and assistance. This is the correct answer.

On site, why was OSHA established?
  • A. On site, to help assure safe and healthful working conditions for workers
  • B. On site, to approve every construction schedule before work begins
  • C. On site, to guarantee construction work has no remaining hazards
  • D. On site, to replace every employer safety program with a federal safety manual

OSHA’s core purpose is to help assure safe and healthful working conditions through standards, enforcement, training, outreach, education, and assistance. This is the correct answer.

What should OSHA 30-Hour Construction training emphasize?
  • A. Learning how to avoid reporting injuries
  • B. Hazard identification, avoidance, control, and prevention
  • C. Replacing all task-specific training required by OSHA standards
  • D. Memorizing every paragraph number in OSHA standards

Outreach construction training is intended to emphasize recognizing, avoiding, controlling, and preventing workplace hazards. This is the correct answer.

Which statement about OSHA purpose and coverage is most accurate? What should OSHA 30-Hour Construction training emphasize?
  • A. On site, replacing all task-specific training required by OSHA standards
  • B. On site, hazard identification, avoidance, control, and prevention
  • C. On site, memorizing every paragraph number in OSHA standards
  • D. On site, learning how to avoid reporting injuries

Outreach construction training is intended to emphasize recognizing, avoiding, controlling, and preventing workplace hazards. This is the correct answer.

A worker asks for access to exposure records related to workplace health hazards. What should the employer do?
  • A. Deny access because health hazards are handled only by management
  • B. Provide access to applicable exposure records as required
  • C. Allow access only after the worker withdraws a complaint
  • D. Replace exposure records with verbal reassurance

Workers have rights to access certain exposure and medical records relevant to workplace health hazards. This is the correct answer.

During an OSHA 30-Hour Construction class, a supervisor asks about OSHA purpose and coverage. Which statement best describes OSHA’s role in construction safety?
  • A. OSHA purchases protective equipment for each employer
  • B. OSHA performs daily supervision for every construction crew
  • C. OSHA establishes and enforces safety and health requirements and provides assistance and education
  • D. OSHA issues building permits for construction projects

OSHA uses standards, enforcement, outreach, education, and assistance to support safe and healthful workplaces. This is the correct answer.

  • B. On site, OSHA issues building permits for construction projects
  • C. On site, OSHA establishes and enforces safety and health requirements and provides assistance and education
  • D. On site, OSHA purchases protective equipment for each employer

OSHA uses standards, enforcement, outreach, education, and assistance to support safe and healthful workplaces. This is the correct answer.

Which construction worker is most likely covered by OSHA protections?
  • A. A self-employed worker with no employees
  • B. A family member doing personal work at home
  • C. A public employee in every state under federal OSHA rules
  • D. An employee working for a private construction contractor

OSHA generally covers private-sector workers and employers, including construction employers, unless a specific exception or state plan structure applies. This is the correct answer.

Key Terms in This Domain

  • OSH Act: The 1970 federal law that created OSHA and established worker safety rights and employer duties
  • Employer responsibilities: Provide a safe workplace, free PPE where required, training in a language workers understand, and report severe injuries to OSHA
  • Whistleblower protection: Section 11(c) prohibits retaliation against workers who exercise OSHA-protected rights
  • Worker rights: Right to safe workplace, to receive training, to access injury logs and SDSs, to file a complaint, and to be free from retaliation
  • OSHA inspection priorities: Imminent danger > severe injuries/fatalities > complaints/referrals > programmed inspections > follow-ups
  • Recordkeeping (1904): 29 CFR 1904: covered employers log work-related injuries and illnesses on OSHA 300, 300A, 301
  • Employer-paid PPE: OSHA generally requires employers to provide and pay for PPE at no cost to workers, with limited exceptions
  • OSHA complaint: A worker may file a confidential complaint requesting an OSHA inspection of a hazardous workplace
  • OSHA Form 300: Log of Work-Related Injuries and Illnesses that covered employers must maintain
  • Stop work authority: Empowering any worker to halt work that they believe is unsafe, without fear of retaliation
