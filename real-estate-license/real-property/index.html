Sample Questions

The metes and bounds method of legal description defines a parcel of land by:

Answer: courses and distances starting and ending at a designated point of beginning

Metes and bounds traces the property boundary using compass bearings (courses) and measured distances, starting and ending at the same point of beginning.

In the rectangular (government) survey system, one section of land contains:

Answer: 640 acres

A section is one square mile (5,280 × 5,280 feet = 27,878,400 square feet ÷ 43,560 = 640 acres).

A lot and block legal description identifies a parcel by:

Answer: referring to a specific lot and block number within a recorded subdivision plat

Lot and block (also called plat-and-lot) descriptions refer to a lot number and block number within a subdivision plat that has been surveyed and recorded in the public records.
