Sample Questions

Which condition is a health hazard in construction?

Answer: Exposure to dust, fumes, noise, heat, chemicals, or other agents that can harm health

Construction health hazards include harmful exposures that may cause illness or injury over time or immediately. This is the correct answer.

What should workers understand about exposure?

Answer: Short or repeated exposures can still be harmful depending on the hazard and dose

Health risk depends on the hazard, amount, duration, and route of exposure. This is the correct answer.

What should a worker do before using an unfamiliar chemical product?

Answer: Review the label, instructions, and safety information and ask questions if unclear

Workers should understand hazards and controls before exposure. This is the correct answer.
