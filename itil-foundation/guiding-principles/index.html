Sample Questions

Which statement best describes the ITIL guiding principles?
  • A. Recommendations that can guide an organization in all circumstances, regardless of changes in goals, strategies, work, or management structure
  • B. Mandatory step-by-step procedures for every service management activity
  • C. A set of practices used only by the service desk
  • D. Measures used only to calculate service provider performance

The guiding principles are universal recommendations that support decisions and actions across an organization. This is the correct answer.

Identify the missing word(s): The guiding principle [?] is most relevant. Which statement best describes the ITIL guiding principles?

  • B. A collection of practices used only by the service desk
  • C. Measures applied solely to calculate service provider performance
  • D. Strict step-by-step procedures for every service management activity

The guiding principles are universal recommendations that support decisions and actions across an organization. This is the correct answer.

The guiding principles interact with each other and should be considered together when making decisions. This is the correct answer.

Key Terms in This Domain

  • Progress iteratively with feedback: Guiding principle: organize work into smaller, manageable iterations with frequent feedback
  • Collaborate and promote visibility: Guiding principle: work together across boundaries; transparency builds trust and better decisions
  • Focus on value: Guiding principle: every activity contributes to value for stakeholders
  • Start where you are: Guiding principle: assess current state and reuse what already works before adopting new methods
  • Think and work holistically: Guiding principle: no service or component stands alone: coordinate the SVS as a whole
  • Optimize and automate: Guiding principle: optimize human effort first, then automate where appropriate
  • Service: A means of enabling value co-creation by facilitating outcomes the customer wants without managing specific costs or risks
  • Service management: A set of specialized organizational capabilities for enabling value for customers in the form of services
  • Value: The perceived benefits, usefulness, and importance of something
  • Service relationship management: Joint activities between provider and consumer to ensure continual value co-creation
