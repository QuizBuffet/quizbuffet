Sample Questions

Which are the four dimensions of service management in ITIL 4?
  • A. Organizations and people; information and technology; partners and suppliers; value streams and processes
  • B. Plan, improve, engage, and deliver and support
  • C. Incident, problem, change, and service request
  • D. Utility, warranty, cost, and risk

The four dimensions are organizations and people, information and technology, partners and suppliers, and value streams and processes. This is the correct answer.

Which statement about four dimensions of service management is CORRECT?
  • A. Organizations and people; information and technology; partners and suppliers; value streams and processes
  • B. Incident, problem, change, and service request
  • C. Utility, warranty, cost, and risk
  • D. Plan, improve, engage, and deliver and support

The four dimensions are organizations and people, information and technology, partners and suppliers, and value streams and processes. This is the correct answer.

Why should all four dimensions be considered?
  • A. To replace the guiding principles with a mandatory checklist
  • B. To ensure a holistic approach to service management and avoid unmanaged constraints
  • C. To make every service use the same supplier and the same process design
  • D. To ensure that technology decisions are made without considering people or suppliers

Considering all four dimensions helps organizations manage services holistically. This is the correct answer.

  • D. Workflow steps and process inputs and outputs

The organizations and people dimension includes human, structural, cultural, and capability aspects. This is the correct answer.

Key Terms in This Domain

  • Information and technology: Dimension covering the information and technology used in service management and service delivery
  • Value streams and processes: Dimension covering the activities, workflows, controls, and procedures needed to achieve agreed objectives
  • Service management: A set of specialized organizational capabilities for enabling value for customers in the form of services
  • Service relationship management: Joint activities between provider and consumer to ensure continual value co-creation
  • Organizations and people: Dimension covering culture, roles, responsibilities, and authority structures
  • Partners and suppliers: Dimension covering relationships with third parties involved in service design, delivery, support, and use
  • Plan (SVC activity): Ensures shared understanding of vision, current status, and improvement direction across all four dimensions
  • Information security management: Practice: protect the information needed by the organization to conduct its business
  • Supplier management: Practice: ensure suppliers and their performance are managed to support seamless quality services
  • IT asset management: Practice: plan and manage the full lifecycle of IT assets to maximize value, control costs, manage risks
