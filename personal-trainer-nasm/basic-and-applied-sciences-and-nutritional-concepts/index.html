Sample Questions

A personal trainer is explaining to a client how the body coordinates movement during exercise. According to NASM, which system is responsible for collecting, processing, and responding to information from the environment?

Answer: The nervous system

NASM defines the nervous system as the system responsible for collecting, processing, and responding to information from the environment.

A personal trainer is studying the two major divisions of the nervous system. According to NASM, which structures make up the central nervous system?

Answer: The brain and spinal cord

NASM identifies the central nervous system as consisting of the brain and spinal cord.

A personal trainer describes the peripheral nervous system to a client who wants to understand how their body senses and moves. According to NASM, which description best defines the peripheral nervous system?

Answer: All the nerves outside the brain and spinal cord that relay sensory and motor information between the CNS and the body

NASM defines the peripheral nervous system as all the nerves outside the brain and spinal cord that relay sensory information from the body to the CNS and motor commands from the CNS to the body.
