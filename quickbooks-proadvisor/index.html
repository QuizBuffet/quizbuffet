The QuickBooks ProAdvisor program is Intuit's free certification for accounting and bookkeeping professionals. The QuickBooks Online (QBO) Certification exam tests practical knowledge of subscription selection, navigation and company settings, list and product/service management, the chart of accounts, customer and sales workflows, vendor and purchase workflows, banking and bank-feed reconciliation, reports, and payroll basics. Certification is delivered through training courses on QuickBooks Online Accountant; candidates pass each module quiz at 80% and the final exam at 80%, with three attempts per module. The Advanced ProAdvisor Certification builds on the basic certification with deeper QBO functionality and advisory workflows.

Common pitfalls

The three traps that kill QuickBooks ProAdvisor candidates: (1) over-memorizing acronyms instead of practicing the application of concepts in scenarios, when the exam is scenario-driven, not a vocab quiz. QuickBooks ProAdvisor questions, answered

Is the QuickBooks ProAdvisor certification free?

Yes. The QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor certification is free through the ProAdvisor program once you have a QuickBooks Online Accountant account, which is also free to create.

How do I get QuickBooks ProAdvisor certification for free?

Sign up for a free QuickBooks Online Accountant account, then go to the ProAdvisor tab and start the QBO Certification training course. Complete each module and pass the module quizzes and final exam at 80% or better.

What is on the ProAdvisor exam?

The QBO Certification covers setup and subscriptions, navigation and settings, list management, the chart of accounts, customer and sales workflows, vendor and purchase workflows, banking and reconciliation, reports, and payroll basics, roughly 50 questions per module, with an 80% passing score and 3 attempts per module.

How long does the ProAdvisor exam take?

There is no strict time limit on most modules, but plan for several hours total since the certification is split across multiple modules with their own quizzes plus a final exam.

Do you need to be an accountant?

No. Anyone can create a free QuickBooks Online Accountant account and complete the ProAdvisor certification, though it is designed for bookkeepers, accountants, and QuickBooks consultants.

How long is ProAdvisor certification valid?

ProAdvisor certifications require annual recertification. Intuit notifies you when your certification exam needs to be retaken to stay current.

What is the difference between ProAdvisor and Advanced ProAdvisor?

The base QBO ProAdvisor certification covers core QuickBooks Online functionality. Advanced ProAdvisor Certification builds on it with deeper QBO features, complex workflows, and advisory-level skills, and is only open to ProAdvisors who already hold the base certification.

Is there a QuickBooks proficiency test?

The QBO Certification exam functions as that proficiency test: it validates hands-on QuickBooks Online skills across setup, transactions, reconciliation, reports, and payroll rather than testing accounting theory alone.
